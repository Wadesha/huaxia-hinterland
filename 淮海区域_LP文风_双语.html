淮海区域

The Huaihai Region

01

先别翻攻略，去站台上闻闻Smell it before the guide

淮海不是一张能在地图上圈得很圆的名片。它散在江苏、山东、河南、安徽四省交界的黄淮平原上，以徐州为心，往外一圈是连云港、淮安、宿迁、蚌埠、商丘、济宁。可你要是真来一趟，会发现这一片有种别处没有的混血气质——北方的豪横、南方的精细，还有两千年汉风夯在骨头里。

我第一次懂淮海，是在徐州东站。高铁门一开，热气混着葱蒜味扑过来，行李箱轮子碾过花岗岩的闷响，像有人在你耳边说：到了。出站口蹲着卖饣它汤的老汉，勺子敲着桶沿，那声音比任何导游词都准。

往南四十公里是淮安，往北一百公里是济宁，往西翻过省界就是商丘的古城墙。它们不抢戏，各守各的滋味。你不必一次吃完——淮海的好处，恰恰是要你慢慢来，一口一口尝。

当地贴士：把徐州当圆心，别贪多。这一圈城市高铁互达多在 1 小时内，住一地、当日往返周边，比拖着箱子连轴转舒服得多。

Huaihai is not a name you can circle neatly on a map. It spills across the Huang–Huai plain where Jiangsu, Shandong, Henan and Anhui meet, pivoting on Xuzhou, flung outward to Lianyungang, Huai'an, Suqian, Bengbu, Shangqiu and Jining. Spend a few days here, though, and you'll taste a hybrid nowhere else has — northern swagger, southern finesse, and two thousand years of Han dynasty bone-deep in the soil.

I understood Huaihai first at Xuzhou East Station. The train doors parted, heat and the smell of scallion and garlic rolled in, suitcase wheels drumming on granite like someone whispering: you're here. An old man crouched by the exit selling sha soup, his ladle tapping the bucket rim — more accurate than any guidebook.

Forty kilometres south is Huai'an; a hundred north, Jining; cross the provincial line west and you hit Shangqiu's old city wall. None of them show off. They keep to their own flavours. You don't have to eat the whole region at once — Huaihai's gift is that it asks you to come back, bite by bite.

Local tip: Treat Xuzhou as your hub and don't overreach. The surrounding cities are mostly within an hour by high-speed rail — stay put, day-trip the neighbours, and spare yourself the suitcase shuffle.
02

淮海到底是什么What Huaihai actually is

淮海这个词，课本上说得轻巧——淮河和黄海之间。可真站在这片土地上，你会发现它比定义胖得多。它既是地理的，也是文化的：黄河夺淮入海那几百年，把南北的脾气搅成一锅；楚汉相争的故事从这片土地的垓下讲起；淮海战役又让"淮海"二字成了现代中国的注脚。

今天你来的淮海，是四省交界的一圈城市，它们共享一种"不东不西、又南又北"的暧昧。吃的既爱葱蒜的冲，也爱蟹粉的鲜；说话既带北方的硬，也带水乡的软。这种混血不是凑出来的，是两千年的兵火、漕运、移民一层层煨出来的。

所以别试图用一句口号概括它。淮海最有意思的地方，恰恰是它不肯被一句话说死——你得多待几天，让舌头和耳朵先认得它，脑子才跟得上。

实用贴士：想快速建立坐标感，先看一眼"淮河—黄河故道—京杭运河"三条线怎么穿过这片平原，之后每座城市的脾气你都能猜个七八分。

Huaihai is a word textbooks say lightly — between the Huai River and the Yellow Sea. Stand on the soil, though, and you'll find it fatter than its definition. It is geography and culture both: the centuries the Yellow River hijacked the Huai and poured to the sea churned north and south into one pot; the Chu–Han war opens at Gaixia on this ground; the Huaihai Campaign made the name a footnote of modern China.

The Huaihai you visit is a ring of cities straddling four provinces, sharing a neither-here-nor-there, both-north-and-south ambiguity. The food loves the punch of scallion and garlic yet also the finesse of crab roe; the speech carries the north's hardness and the water towns' softness. This hybrid wasn't assembled — it was slow-simmered by two thousand years of war, canals and migration.

So don't try to sum it in a slogan. Huaihai's best trick is refusing to be said in one line — you stay a few days, let tongue and ear know it first, and the head catches up later.

Handy tip: To get your bearings fast, trace how three lines cut this plain — the Huai River, the Yellow River's old course, the Grand Canal — and you'll guess most cities' temper right.
03

怎么走Getting around

淮海是中国的铁路肚脐。徐州东站是京沪与徐兰高铁的交叉点，去哪都方便得有点不像话。市内地铁两条线刚够用，打车便宜，共享单车满街。连云港靠海，多坐一趟公交去连岛，比跟团省心。

蚌埠、宿迁、商丘这些城市不大，腿着就能逛完老城核心。真要跨省，高铁票提前一天买就够，节假日才需抢。

实用贴士：下载「铁路 12306」官方 App 买票最稳，别在第三方平台多花冤枉钱；徐州东站巨大，留足换乘步行时间。

Huaihai is China's railway belly-button. Xuzhou East is where the Beijing–Shanghai and Xuzhou–Lanzhou lines cross, so getting anywhere is almost absurdly easy. Two metro lines cover the city centre, cabs are cheap, shared bikes are everywhere. Lianyungang sits on the sea — take a bus out to Lian Island and skip the tour groups.

Bengbu, Suqian and Shangqiu are compact; you can walk their old cores on foot. Cross-province, buy rail tickets a day ahead and you're fine — only holidays call for a scramble.

Handy tip: Use the official 12306 app for tickets and skip the markups of resellers; Xuzhou East is enormous, so budget walking time between transfers.
04

徐州：最不掩饰北方血统的心脏Xuzhou: the unashamed northern heart

徐州是淮海的心脏，也是它最不掩饰自己北方血统的地方。云龙山下，汉代的石头还躺着——楚王山、龟山、北洞山，一座城压着好几座汉墓。市中心的彭城广场地下是地铁，地上是烧烤摊，时间的层次就这么叠着。

徐州人说话硬，喝酒更硬。夜市里一把把羊肉串在炭上卷着烟，配一瓶本地啤酒，是这座城最诚实的自我介绍。想安静，就上云龙山，城门楼子往外望，一片城廓和远处的煤都天际线，历史的厚度和当下的油温混在一起。

博物馆的汉兵马俑比西安的小，却近得能看见眉眼;汉画像石馆把两千年前的市井刻在石头上——车马、乐舞、厨房，古人过的日子跟你想象的不一样。

当地贴士：龟山汉墓的甬道直得邪门，是工匠拿肉眼量的，请别伸手摸石壁;博物馆多周一闭馆，去前查一眼开放日。

Xuzhou is Huaihai's heart and the least shy about its northern blood. Below Yunlong Mountain the Han stones still lie — Chu king's hill, Guishan, Beidong, a city sitting atop several Han tombs. At Pengcheng Square the metro runs underground, skewer stalls above; that's how layers of time stack.

Xuzhou people speak hard and drink harder. At the night market mutton skewers curl smoke over coals with a local beer — the city's most honest self-introduction. For quiet, climb Yunlong Mountain; from the gate tower a whole city spreads out with the coal-town skyline beyond, history's weight mixed with tonight's cooking oil.

The museum's Han figurines are smaller than Xi'an's yet close enough to read their brows; the Han stone-carving gallery freezes two-thousand-year-old street life in rock — carts, dancers, kitchens, nothing like you pictured.

Local tip: Guishan's passage is eerily straight, measured by eye — don't touch the stone; many museums close Mondays, so check the open day first.
05

吃什么What to eat

淮海的味道，是铁锅与文火吵出来的一架。徐州地锅鸡，鸡块在生铁锅里炖到将烂，锅沿贴一圈死面饼，吸饱汤汁的那一面嚼着像在吃肉。饣它汤是唤醒一整天的武器——麦仁、鸡汤、胡椒，一碗下肚，整个人被点亮。把子肉是卤得油亮的方肉，夹进馒头，是劳动者的午餐哲学。

往南到淮安，画风一转：这里是淮扬菜的发源地之一。文楼汤包咬开先吸汤，软兜长鱼嫩得不像话。再往西，淮南八公山说豆腐是这儿发明的——刘安炼丹炼出了豆腐，算是中国人最温柔的意外。宿迁的洋河酒，蓝瓶子里装着江淮平原的粮食香。

当地贴士：地锅鸡认准老小区巷子里的夫妻店，比商场连锁香;汤包别急着咬，先戳个小口吹凉、吸汤，不然烫的是舌头。

Huaihai flavour is an argument fought between a cast-iron wok and a low flame. Xuzhou's diguo ji — chicken braised until tender in a raw-iron pot, a ring of flatbread pasted to the rim soaking up the gravy, the gravy-side chewy as meat. Sha soup is a dawn weapon: wheat kernels, chicken broth, pepper, one bowl and you're switched on. Bazirou is glossy braised pork squared into a bun — the lunch philosophy of labourers.

South in Huai'an the mood turns refined: this is a birthplace of Huaiyang cuisine. At Wenlou, bite the soup dumpling, sip first. Soft eel is unreal. West again, at Bagongshan in Huainan, they'll tell you tofu was invented here — Liu An was chasing immortality and got China's gentlest accident. Suqian's Yanghe liquor hides the grain-scent of the Jianghuai plain in a blue bottle.

Local tip: For diguo ji, trust the husband-wife joints in old neighbourhood lanes over mall chains; with soup dumplings, poke a small hole, blow, sip — or burn your tongue.
06

淮安：淮扬菜的亲妈Huai'an: mother of Huaiyang cuisine

淮安是淮扬菜的亲妈，低调得有点委屈。河下古镇的青石板被多少双软底鞋磨亮，已经数不清;吴承恩在这儿写完了孙悟空。文楼汤包是手艺的炫技——皮薄得透光，汤多得一戳就塌。

漕运博物馆讲的是一个被运河养大的城市怎么老去又怎么新生。站在里运河边，你会明白淮安的富贵不是摆在脸上的，是沉在水底、刻在闸口的。想吃点素的，去镇淮楼旁的茶社，一笼蒸饺配一壶碧螺春，时间会慢下来。

淮安人讲究"不时不食"，什么节气吃什么，比日历还准。你跟着本地人的筷子走，就不会错。

当地贴士：河下古镇免费，巷子随便钻;文楼汤包饭点要排队，赶早别赶晚，凉了汤就收了劲。

Huai'an is the mother of Huaiyang cuisine, modest to a fault. Hexia's flagstones, polished by how many soft-soled shoes, no one counts; Wu Cheng'en finished Monkey King here. Wenlou's soup dumplings are a flex of craft — skin thin as light, broth so full one poke and it collapses.

The Canal Transport Museum tells how a city raised by the Grand Canal aged and renewed. Stand by the Inner Canal and you'll see Huai'an's wealth isn't worn on the face — it sinks in the water, carved in the lock gates. For something light, a tea house by Zhenhuai Tower, a steamer of dumplings with a pot of Biluochun, and time slows.

Huai'an people hold to "eat by the season" — what to eat when is truer than any calendar. Follow a local's chopsticks and you won't go wrong.

Local tip: Hexia ancient town is free, lanes yours to wander; Wenlou dumplings queue at meal peaks, go early not late — cold, the broth loses its nerve.
07

连云港：淮海唯一正经靠海的城市Lianyungang: the only real coast

连云港是淮海唯一正经靠海的城市，所以当地人一提海鲜眼睛就亮。花果山是招牌，可我更劝你去连岛——大堤把岛和岸缝在一起，步行道免费，海风免费，夕阳把防波堤染成橘色时，没人想起工作。

海鲜大排档的生蚝十块钱一打，老板娘手一抖就多给俩。梭子蟹肥的季节，一桌人围着盆抢，壳堆成小山。想看港口的另一种壮阔，去墟沟，货轮排着队进港，汽笛声把海雾撕开一道口子。

连云港的底色是移民城市，口音杂，脾气直，陌生人同桌也能碰一杯。这种海边的豪爽，是淮海别处学不来的。

实用贴士：连岛大堤步行道不收钱，海风免费;花果山的猴子真会抢塑料袋，随身零食收好。

Lianyungang is the only city in Huaihai with a serious coast, so locals' eyes light up at seafood. Huaguo Mountain is the headline draw, but I'd steer you to Lian Island — the causeway stitches island to shore, the path free, the sea breeze free, and when dusk turns the breakwater orange nobody thinks of work.

Oyster stalls sell a dozen for ten yuan, and the owner's hand slips an extra two. In swimming-crab season a table fights over a basin, shells piled to a hill. For another kind of grandeur, go to Xugou — cargo ships queue into port, their horns tearing a slit in the sea mist.

Lianyungang's base is a migrant city: mixed accents, straight tempers, strangers at one table still clink a glass. This seaside warmth is something nowhere else in Huaihai can copy.

Handy tip: Lian Island's seawall path is free, the sea breeze gratis; Huaguo's monkeys genuinely snatch plastic bags, so stash your snacks.
08

宿迁：没什么非要打卡的好处Suqian: the virtue of nowhere urgent

宿迁没什么非要打卡的景点，这恰恰是它的好。项王故里让你记起那个不肯过江东的男人;洋河酒厂的气味隔着马路就能醉。骆马湖边骑车，风把脑子里的表格吹散。

宿迁人说话慢，吃饭也慢，你急不来。不如坐下喝杯酒，听他们讲乾隆下江南为啥老在这儿拐弯——故事真假另说，那种把自个儿家乡当宝贝絮叨的劲儿，本身就好看。

三台山森林公园把丘陵和杉林泡在水里，木栈道绕着湖走，安静得能听见自己的脚步。这种地方不适合赶，适合赖着。

当地贴士：项王故里看的是气节不是热闹;想清静去骆马湖或三台山，工作日去几乎包场。

Suqian has no sights you must tick off — which is exactly its virtue. The Xiang Yu hometown reminds you of the man who wouldn't cross east of the river; the Yanghe distillery's scent drunks you from across the road. Cycle by Luoma Lake and the wind scatters the spreadsheets in your head.

Suqian people speak slow and eat slow; you can't hurry them. So sit, drink, and hear why Qianlong always turned off here on his southern tours — truth aside, the way they fuss over their homeland is worth the price. Santai Mountain Forest Park soaks hills and metasequoia in water; boardwalks ring the lake, quiet enough to hear your own step. A place to linger, not rush.

Local tip: Xiang Yu's hometown is about honour, not bustle; for quiet go Luoma Lake or Santai, near empty on weekdays.
09

三座小城：商丘·蚌埠·淮南Three small cities: Shangqiu · Bengbu · Huainan

商丘的古城墙是圆的，保存得近乎固执，芒砀山的汉墓深得手机没信号，适合想躲清静的人。商丘还是商文明的源头之一，地下埋的比地上看的更老。

蚌埠夹在淮河边，龙子湖傍晚有人跳舞有人钓，南北通衢的火车从不满足睡。这座城是被铁路喂大的，站台上能听见半部现代中国的汽笛。

淮南把"豆腐故里"挂在嘴边，八公山的泉水煮出来的豆腐嫩得要用勺舀——顺带，淮南牛肉汤也是一绝，辣得通透。三座城都不大，却各自有一股不肯被徐州盖过的硬气。

实用贴士：芒砀山墓道深、信号弱，结伴走别落单;淮南牛肉汤配烧饼是本地人的早餐答案，趁热。

Shangqiu's old wall is round, preserved with near stubbornness; Mangdang's Han tombs run so deep your phone loses signal — good for whoever wants quiet. Shangqiu is also a cradle of Shang civilisation; what's buried is older than what's seen.

Bengbu sits on the Huai, Longzi Lake at dusk full of dancers and anglers, the north–south railway never sleeping. This city was raised by rail; on its platforms you hear half the whistle-history of modern China.

Huainan wears "birthplace of tofu" on its sleeve; tofu boiled in Bagongshan spring is so tender you spoon it — and by the way, Huainan beef soup is also supreme, cleanly hot. None of the three is large, yet each holds a hardness refusing to be overshadowed by Xuzhou.

Handy tip: Mangdang's passages are deep and signal-poor — go with company, don't straggle; Huainan beef soup with a sesame cake is the local breakfast answer, eat it hot.
10

懂点规矩Know the ropes

淮海人认根。徐州满城汉墓，龟山汉墓的甬道直得邪门，是两千年前工匠拿肉眼量的——请别伸手去摸，石壁怕汗。酒桌上敬酒有讲究：晚辈杯沿要低过长辈，这是礼数不是客套。

淮海战役纪念馆在徐州，气氛是沉的。进去把手机调静音，别在展馆里大笑喧哗——这片土地记得的事，比导游词重。

实用贴士：方言隔市就变，徐州话硬、淮安话软;听不懂就笑一笑，locals 不介意你问「这句啥意思」。

Huaihai people honour roots. Xuzhou is riddled with Han tombs; the passage at Guishan is eerily straight — two-thousand-year-old craftsmen measured it by eye. Don't reach out and touch; the stone dislikes sweat. At the table, toasting has rules: a junior's cup rim stays below an elder's. That's etiquette, not fuss.

The Huaihai Campaign Memorial Hall in Xuzhou is solemn. Silence your phone; no laughing in the galleries. This land remembers things heavier than any caption.

Handy tip: The dialect shifts city to city — Xuzhou's is hard, Huai'an's soft. If you don't follow, just smile; locals won't mind you asking what a phrase means.
11

什么时候去When to go

春秋是淮海最体面的季节。四月徐州满城梧桐新绿，云龙湖边跑步的人比游客多;十月淮安的蟹黄汤包正当令，空气里都是甜的。夏天湿热，雷阵雨说来就来，好处是西瓜便宜得离谱。冬天灰蒙，但若赶上一场雪，龟山汉墓的石阶白得肃穆，另有一番气。

当地贴士：想看牡丹别跑洛阳，菏泽的曹州牡丹园规模不输，人却少一半;想看水浒，去济宁梁山，比影视城真。

Spring and autumn are Huaihai's decent seasons. In April Xuzhou greens with new sycamore trees, more runners than tourists by Yunlong Lake; by October Huai'an's crab-roe dumplings are in season, the air almost sweet. Summer is humid, storms arrive unannounced, but watermelons are ridiculously cheap. Winter is grey — yet after snow, Guishan's stone steps turn solemn white, a mood all its own.

Local tip: Skip Luoyang for peonies — Heze's Caozhou garden matches it with half the crowd; for Water Margin, go to Liangshan in Jining, realer than any film set.
12

去哪悠走Where to wander

云龙湖是徐州的肺。天没亮透时绕湖走半圈，能看到市民打太极、钓鱼、念叨股票——一座城市最本真的活气都在这儿。龟山汉墓值得为那条直甬道专程来一次。连云港花果山被《西游记》供成圣地，山顶看海，半山看猴，别较真哪块石头是孙悟空的。

宿迁项王故里纪念那个不肯过江东的楚霸王;商丘芒砀山的汉梁王墓群，墓道深得手机信号都断;微山湖边还能听见铁道游击队的调子。淮河自己不声不响，从桐柏山流到黄海，养活了半個东部。

实用贴士：云龙湖免费、全天开放，傍晚去最舒服;花果山的猴子真会抢塑料袋，随身零食收好。

Yunlong Lake is Xuzhou's lung. Walk half its loop before the mist clears and you'll catch citizens tai-chi-ing, fishing, grumbling about stocks — a whole city's dawn in one ring. Guishan Han Tomb earns a trip for that dead-straight passage alone. Lianyungang's Huaguo Mountain is sacred to Journey to the West; sea from the top, monkeys halfway — don't fret which rock was Sun Wukong.

Suqian's Xiang Yu hometown remembers the king of Chu who wouldn't cross the river east. Shangqiu's Mangdang Mountain hides Han royal tombs so deep your signal dies. By Weishan Lake you can still hear the Railway Guerrillas' tune. The Huai River itself says nothing, flowing from Tongbai to the Yellow Sea, feeding half the east.

Handy tip: Yunlong Lake is free and open all day — dusk is best; Huaguo's monkeys genuinely snatch plastic bags, so stash your snacks.
13

不花钱也值得Worth it free

淮海最贵的东西，大多不要钱。云龙湖的风、户部山老民居的砖雕、运河边傍晚的散步道，全是白给的。徐州博物馆常设展免费，汉兵马俑比西安的小，却近得能看见眉眼。淮安河下古镇的巷子随便钻，一砖一瓦都是明清的呼吸。

连岛的大堤步行道不收钱，海风免费;蚌埠龙子湖傍晚有人跳舞、有人钓鱼，你站着看就是参与。淮海教会我一件事：好地方不靠门票证明自己。

当地贴士：博物馆多周一闭馆，别扑空;想省，把门票钱留给药王街的夜市小吃，更值。

Huaihai's priciest things mostly cost nothing. The wind off Yunlong Lake, the brick carvings of Hubu Shan's old homes, the canal-side promenade at dusk — all free. Xuzhou Museum's permanent galleries are free; the Han figurines are smaller than Xi'an's yet close enough to read their brows. Huai'an's Hexia ancient town lanes are yours to wander, every tile breathing Ming and Qing.

Lian Island's seawall path is free, the sea breeze gratis; at Bengbu's Longzi Lake locals dance and fish at dusk — stand and watch and you're in it. Huaihai taught me one thing: good places don't prove themselves with a ticket.

Local tip: Many museums close Mondays — don't arrive to a locked door; save ticket money for Yaowang Street's night-market snacks, better spent.
14

收束：再来时，记得空着肚子Before you leave: come hungry next time

淮海不会在第一眼就讨好你。它不像江南那样把美摆上桌，也不像北方那样把气势拍你脸上。它慢，它混，它认死理也讲人情。

你逛过徐州的一块汉砖、淮安的一口汤包、连云港的一段海堤，临走会明白：这片土地最舍得给的，从来不是景点的名气，而是那种"你来了，就当自己人"的熟络。它不急着留你，却总让你惦记。

所以别想着一次看全。淮海是那种要你回头的地方——回头越多，它越把真东西亮给你。下次来，还请空着肚子、带着好奇。

Huaihai won't win you at first glance. It doesn't plate beauty like Jiangnan, nor slam grandeur in your face like the north. It's slow, it's mixed, it holds its ground yet keeps its warmth.

You'll touch one Han brick in Xuzhou, one soup dumpling in Huai'an, one seawall in Lianyungang, and leaving you'll get it: what this land gives most freely was never a sight's fame, but the ease of being treated as one of its own. It won't rush to keep you, yet always leaves you missing it.

So don't expect to see it all at once. Huaihai is a place that asks you back — the more you return, the more it shows its real self. Next time, come hungry, come curious.
