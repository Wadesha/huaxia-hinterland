襄阳

Xiangyang

一座没被大江大角色过的城——郭靖守过的襄阳，就在汉江拐弯的地方。

A city no great river or mountain upstaged — the Xiangyang Guo Jing held stands where the Han River bends.

01

先别奔古隆中，去汉江边坐坐Arrive by the Han River

襄阳不抢戏。你一下高铁，城新得没脾气，可往北到汉江边，古城墙猛地亮出来——一座完整的明代砖城，枕着汉水，像大江怀里的一枚钉。

我第一次懂襄阳，是在汉江的傍晚。护城河的水慢吞吞转，城楼影子在江里晃，远处有人在唱湖北大鼓。那一刻你明白，这座城把"守"字写进了砖。

当地贴士：以襄阳古城(临汉门一带)为基地，古隆中、唐城都在 1 小时内；住城内，江边最舒服。
Local tip:Base in the old town (by Linhan Gate); Gulongzhong and Tang Town within an hour — stay inside, riverside best.

Xiangyang doesn't show off. Step off the train and the city is new without temper, yet north at the Han River the old wall lights sudden — a complete Ming brick city, pillow on the Han, a nail in the great river's arms.

I got Xiangyang at dusk on the Han. The moat turns slow, the gate tower's shadow sways on the river, someone sang the Hubei drum. That moment you see this city wrote 'hold' into its brick.

当地贴士：以襄阳古城(临汉门一带)为基地，古隆中、唐城都在 1 小时内；住城内，江边最舒服。
Local tip:Base in the old town (by Linhan Gate); Gulongzhong and Tang Town within an hour — stay inside, riverside best.
02

襄阳到底是什么What Xiangyang is

襄阳是三国之城，郭靖黄蓉守襄阳的故事让这座城成了"侠"的代名词。它也是汉江中游的枢纽，南北货在此转，古城枕江而建，易守难攻。

唐城影视基地是后来建的，拍过不少大制作，可襄阳人自己说，真三国在城墙的砖缝里，不在摄影棚。

Xiangyang is the Three Kingdoms city; Guo Jing and Huang Rong holding Xiangyang made it stand for 'chivalry'. It's also the Han's midstream hub, where north-south goods turn, the old town built on the river, easy to hold hard to take.

The Tang Town film base was built later, shot many big productions, yet Xiangyang folk say the real Three Kingdoms sits in the wall's brick joints, not the sound stage.

03

怎么走Getting around

襄阳东站在汉十与郑渝线上，从武汉过来高铁两小时出头，从郑州过来约三小时。市内公交慢，打车便宜；古城在汉江北岸，腿着就能逛。古隆中在城西，有公交。

汉江边步道免费，骑车最舒服，不用车。

实用贴士：买票认准官方「铁路 12306」App；古城步行最舒服，别开车进胡同。
Handy tip:Use the official 12306 app; the old town walks best, don't drive into lanes.

Xiangyang East on the Wuhan–Shiyan and Zhengzhou–Chongqing lines is just over two hours from Wuhan, about three from Zhengzhou. City buses slow, cabs cheap; the old town north of the Han is walkable. Gulongzhong west of town has a bus.

The Han riverside path is free, bike best, no car needed.

实用贴士：买票认准官方「铁路 12306」App；古城步行最舒服，别开车进胡同。
Handy tip:Use the official 12306 app; the old town walks best, don't drive into lanes.
04

襄阳古城与汉江The old town and the Han

襄阳古城是保存完整的明代砖城，临汉门、夫人城都在线上。城墙可登，沿汉江走，对岸的岘山把城衬得小。护城河宽，是国内最宽的几座之一。

江边帆船少，可渡口还在，傍晚有老人钓鱼、唱戏。你不用买票，沿墙走一圈就是半部南北战争史。

当地贴士：城墙登临看江景；江边步道免费，傍晚去最舒服。
Local tip:Climb the wall for the river view; the riverside path is free, best at dusk.

The Xiangyang old town is a complete Ming brick city; Linhan Gate and the Wife City line it. The wall is climbable along the Han; across, Xian Mountain makes the town small. The moat is wide — among China's broadest.

Few sails now, yet the ferry remains; at dusk old men fish and sing. No ticket needed; walk the wall and you've read half the north-south war history.

当地贴士：城墙登临看江景；江边步道免费，傍晚去最舒服。
Local tip:Climb the wall for the river view; the riverside path is free, best at dusk.
05

古隆中：诸葛亮的草庐Gulongzhong: Zhuge Liang's thatch

古隆中在城西群山里，是诸葛亮"躬耕"的地方。三顾茅庐的戏从这儿起，草庐、抱膝亭、武侯祠顺着山势铺。别较真哪间是当年的——它是凭吊，不是考古。

山里竹多泉清，溪水凉得激牙。你走在小径上，会懂为什么卧龙选这片静土——乱世里，一处能发呆的山，比一座城金贵。

实用贴士：古隆中门票含祠；山里溪凉带件外套，路滑慢行。
Handy tip:Gulongzhong ticket covers the shrine; the hill stream is cool, a layer, paths slick — slow.

Gulongzhong in the hills west of town is where Zhuge Liang 'tilled'; the three-visits drama starts here, thatch hut, Knee-hug Pavilion, Martial Marquis shrine laid on the slope. Don't fuss which hut is the old one — it's tribute, not archaeology.

Bamboo and clear springs, stream cold to the teeth. Walk the path and get why the Sleeping Dragon chose this quiet soil — in chaos, a hill to daydream beats a city.

实用贴士：古隆中门票含祠；山里溪凉带件外套，路滑慢行。
Handy tip:Gulongzhong ticket covers the shrine; the hill stream is cool, a layer, paths slick — slow.
06

吃什么What to eat

襄阳的味道在汉江和码头的汤里。牛肉面（牛杂面）是招牌，红汤辣得通透，配黄酒是本地人的汤哲学。缠蹄是卤得油亮的猪蹄卷，凉吃下酒最妙。

孔明菜是本地腌菜，配粥下饭都行。夜市便宜管饱，街头一口锅能香半条街。

当地贴士：牛肉面认老店，辣度可点；黄酒趁温喝，凉了涩。
Local tip:Beef noodle from old names, heat to taste; yellow wine warm, cool turns astringent.

Xiangyang's flavour is in the Han and dock soup. Beef noodle (mixed offal) is the signboard — red broth cleanly hot, with yellow wine the locals' soup philosophy. Wrapped trotter is glossy braised roll, best cold with liquor.

Kongming vegetable is local pickle, with congee or rice. The night market cheap and filling; one street pot scents half a lane.

当地贴士：牛肉面认老店，辣度可点；黄酒趁温喝，凉了涩。
Local tip:Beef noodle from old names, heat to taste; yellow wine warm, cool turns astringent.
07

懂点规矩Know the ropes

古城墙、古隆中是庄重之地，进门手机静音，别在殿里大声。三国是襄阳的骄傲，可别拿"郭靖是不是真的"去戳——这座城把"守"当骨，你客气点，他们能把每场仗说给你听。

江边钓鱼是市民乐子，你在旁看就行，别抢竿。

The old wall and Gulongzhong are solemn — silence phones, no loud talk in halls. The Three Kingdoms is Xiangyang's pride, yet don't poke with 'was Guo Jing real' — the city takes 'hold' as bone, be polite and they'll tell you each battle.

Riverside angling is citizens' joy; watch, don't grab the rod.

08

什么时候去When to go

春天汉江新绿，古城舒服。秋天天高云淡，岘山配蓝天最好看。夏天热，江边风大稍好。冬天冷，可雪后古城肃穆，城墙白砖黑瓦像一幅水墨。

若为了三国气质，春秋最对；若为了清静，冬天人最少。

实用贴士：夏天江边带水；冬天砖滑，慢行。
Handy tip:Summer riverside, bring water; winter brick slick, go slow.

Spring greens the Han, the old town ease. Autumn high and clear, Xian Mountain with blue sky best. Summer hot, riverside wind a bit better. Winter cold, yet snow on the old town is ink-painting, white brick black tile.

For the Three Kingdoms mood, spring–autumn fit; for quiet, winter fewest.

实用贴士：夏天江边带水；冬天砖滑，慢行。
Handy tip:Summer riverside, bring water; winter brick slick, go slow.
09

去哪悠走Where to wander

汉江边是免费的天然步道，骑车沿江，古城墙、夫人城、渡口都在沿线。城内胡同随便钻，青砖门关着的故事比开着的多。

古隆中小径免费段多，竹泉边站着听风就是半部三国。襄阳的好走，在于江把整座城摁成了一条线。

实用贴士：江边骑车免费；古隆中小径顺沟走别下水。
Handy tip:The riverside bike is free; Gulongzhong paths follow the gully, don't enter water.

The Han riverside is a free path; bike it and the old wall, Wife City, ferry line the way. The inner alleys are yours to wander; more stories behind closed blue-brick doors than open.

Gulongzhong has free path stretches; by the bamboo spring stand and hear wind — half a Three Kingdoms. Xiangyang's joy is the river stringing the city on one line.

实用贴士：江边骑车免费；古隆中小径顺沟走别下水。
Handy tip:The riverside bike is free; Gulongzhong paths follow the gully, don't enter water.
10

不花钱也值得Worth it free

襄阳最贵的东西，大多不要钱。汉江的风、古城在暮色里的轮廓、江边那阵湖北大鼓，站着看就是参与。雪后古城配红灯，比收费园里还好看。

江边大爷们唱戏不收钱，你站着听就是捧场。襄阳教会我：好地方不靠门票证明自己。

当地贴士：江边唱戏免费听；博物馆多周一闭馆，去前查开放日。
Local tip:Riverside opera is free; many museums close Mondays — check first.

Xiangyang's priciest things mostly cost nothing. The Han's wind, the old town's dusk outline, the Hubei drum beyond — stand and you're in it. Snow on the old town with red lamps beats any paid garden.

Old men sing opera free by the river; stand and listen, that's applause. Xiangyang taught me: good places don't prove themselves with a ticket.

当地贴士：江边唱戏免费听；博物馆多周一闭馆，去前查开放日。
Local tip:Riverside opera is free; many museums close Mondays — check first.
11

收束Before you leave

襄阳不会一下子把你看服。它没有名山的名气，也没有水乡的精巧。它稳，它厚，它又侠又守——一座城能扛住南北，一部书能从这起笔。

你逛过汉江的一岸城影、古隆中的一间草庐、江边的一阵鼓，临走会明白：这片土地最舍得给的，从来不是名气，而是那种"守得住"的稳。它不急着留你，却总让你惦记。

Xiangyang won't win you at once. No famous mountain's fame, no water town's finesse. It's steady, deep, chivalrous and holding — one city stood north and south, one book penned here.

You'll see the Han's riverside town-shadow, Gulongzhong's thatch hut, a drum by the river — and leaving you'll get it: what this land gives most freely was never fame, but the steadiness of 'able to hold'. It won't rush to keep you, yet always leaves you missing it.
