枣庄

Zaozhuang

淮海西端的城市，因煤矿和铁道游击队闻名，也是台儿庄大捷的发生地——一座运河古城在战火中毁灭又重建，如今青砖黛瓦下藏着最坚韧的抗日记忆。

A city on Huaihai's west edge, famous for coal and the Railway Guerrillas, and home of the Taierzhuang victory — a canal old town destroyed then rebuilt in war, its blue tiles hiding the toughest anti-Japanese memory.

01

先别奔台儿庄，去运河边上坐Arrive by the canal

枣庄不声不响。你一下高铁，城破得没脾气——旧矿区的烟囱还在，可已经不冒烟了。先到运河边上坐坐，风里有煤渣和旧木头的味道，得先把这座城的"硬"气咽下去。

我第一次懂枣庄，是在台儿庄运河的傍晚。对岸的灯亮了，像一串被擦亮的珍珠，映在水里晃来晃去。那一刻你明白，这座城把最惨的记忆刻在砖上，却把最软的灯挂在了水上。

当地贴士：以台儿庄城区为基地；铁道游击队纪念园在薛城，约一小时车程。
Local tip:Base in Taierzhuang; the Railway Guerrilla memorial is in Xuecheng, about an hour away.

Zaozhuang keeps quiet. Step off and the city feels broken — old mine chimneys still stand, but no smoke comes out. Sit by the canal first — wind carries coal dust and old wood, swallow this city's hardness first.

I got Zaozhuang at dusk on Taierzhuang Canal. Lights came on across the water, a string of polished pearls, shimmering and swaying. That moment you see — the city carved its worst memories into bricks, but hung its softest lights over the water.

当地贴士：以台儿庄城区为基地；铁道游击队纪念园在薛城，约一小时车程。
Local tip:Base in Taierzhuang; the Railway Guerrilla memorial is in Xuecheng, about an hour away.
02

枣庄到底是什么What Zaozhuang is

枣庄是淮海西端的煤城，京沪、陇海两条铁路交汇处。它因抗日出名——1938年台儿庄大捷是抗战初期最重大的胜利，铁道游击队在津浦线上的传奇故事传遍全国。

今天的枣庄一半废墟一半新城，旧矿区在转型，运河古城在重建。枣庄的好，在于它又惨又韧——这座城曾被战争撕成碎片，又自己把它粘起来。

Zaozhuang is a former coal city on Huaihai's west, at the crossing of Beijing–Shanghai and Lianyungang–Lanzhou railways. It's famous for anti-Japanese resistance — the 1938 Taierzhuang victory was the war's first major win, and the Railway Guerrillas' tales on the Tianjin–Pukau line spread nationwide.

Today it's half-ruin, half-new; old mines transform, the canal old town rebuilt. Its grace is tragedy and toughness — torn to bits by war, then glued back together by its own hands.

03

怎么走Getting around

枣庄西站在京沪高铁上，从徐州过来约半小时；台儿庄站更近景区。市区打车便宜，铁道游击队纪念园在薛城区，建议包车或打滴滴。

台儿庄古城很大，建议夜里坐船游览，比白天更有感觉。

实用贴士：台儿庄古城门票含游船；记得穿舒服的鞋，古城里都是石板路。
Handy tip:Taierzhuang ticket includes boat ride; wear comfortable shoes — cobblestones everywhere.

Zaozhuang West on Beijing–Shanghai HSR is half an hour from Xuzhou; Taierzhuang Station closer to the scenic area. Cabs are cheap; the Railway Guerrilla memorial in Xuecheng is best reached by chartered car or Didi.

Taierzhuang old town is huge — take a boat cruise at night, it's better than daytime.

实用贴士：台儿庄古城门票含游船；记得穿舒服的鞋，古城里都是石板路。
Handy tip:Taierzhuang ticket includes boat ride; wear comfortable shoes — cobblestones everywhere.
04

台儿庄大捷纪念馆Taierzhuang Victory Memorial

台儿庄大捷纪念馆坐落在古城西北角，是在当年激战的旧址上建的。馆里有一张巨大的油画，描绘1938年春池峰城师长指挥国军与日军在运河上血战的场景，看了让人沉默。

纪念馆最让人震撼的是一面"尸骨墙"——是当年阵亡将士遗骨的模型堆叠而成的，你站在墙前，能感到一种沉甸甸的重量，压得你喘不过气。

当地贴士：纪念馆免费，凭身份证领票；周一闭馆。
Local tip:Memorial is free, ID for tickets; closed Mondays.

The Taierzhuang Victory Memorial sits in the old town's northwest corner, built on the very site of the 1938 fighting. A giant oil painting depicts General Chi Fengcheng commanding Nationalists against Japanese on the canal; it silences you.

Most staggering is a 'wall of bones' — a stacked memorial of model remains of the fallen. Stand before it and you feel a weight you can barely breathe under.

当地贴士：纪念馆免费，凭身份证领票；周一闭馆。
Local tip:Memorial is free, ID for tickets; closed Mondays.
05

台儿庄运河古城Taierzhuang canal old town

台儿庄运河古城是抗战后重建的，青砖黛瓦、小桥流水，全是按照明清运河古城的格局修的。它不是原样复制——因为原样已经毁了——而是"重建一个能让人记得住的样子"。

夜里古城最好看。红灯笼一排排挂起来，运河上的画舫亮着灯慢慢滑过，有姑娘撑着伞在青石板上走。你可以找个二楼的茶馆坐下来，看着下面的人群，想着八十年前这里曾是一片废墟。

实用贴士：古城门票 118 元，含往返高铁票（枣庄西站—台儿庄）；夜游比白天票略贵。
Handy tip:Old town ticket 118 yuan, includes round-trip HSR from Zaozhuang West; night ticket slightly pricier.

Taierzhuang Canal Old Town was rebuilt after the war — blue tiles, small bridges, water, built to Ming–Qing canal town scale. It's not a copy — the original is gone — but 'rebuilding a shape worth remembering'.

Best at night. Red lanterns hang row by row, painted boats drift with lights, a girl with an umbrella walks over the stones. Sit in a second-floor teahouse, watch the crowd below, and imagine — eighty years ago this was all rubble.

实用贴士：古城门票 118 元，含往返高铁票（枣庄西站—台儿庄）；夜游比白天票略贵。
Handy tip:Old town ticket 118 yuan, includes round-trip HSR from Zaozhuang West; night ticket slightly pricier.
06

铁道游击队纪念园Railway Guerrilla memorial

铁道游击队纪念园在薛城区，建在临城火车站旧址上，就是当年游击队爬火车、杀鬼子的地方。馆里有一面"英雄墙"，刻着一百多位当年游击队队员的名字，他们大多没活过三十岁。

纪念园旁边还保留着一段当年的津浦铁路，现在只用作参观。你可以站在铁轨上，想象八十年前刘洪和鲁汉是怎么扒上火车的。

当地贴士：纪念园免费开放；周边有游击队主题餐馆，菜名都是《铁道游击队》里的人名。
Local tip:Memorial is free; nearby guerrilla-themed restaurants name dishes after characters from the novel.

The Railway Guerrilla memorial is in Xuecheng, built on the old Lincheng railway station — the very place they climbed trains and killed Japanese soldiers. Inside is a 'hero wall' with more than one hundred guerrilla names, most dead before thirty.

A stretch of the old Tianjin–Pukau line sits beside the memorial, now for visitors only. Stand on the rails and imagine how Liu Hong and Lu Han climbed aboard those trains eighty years ago.

当地贴士：纪念园免费开放；周边有游击队主题餐馆，菜名都是《铁道游击队》里的人名。
Local tip:Memorial is free; nearby guerrilla-themed restaurants name dishes after characters from the novel.
07

抱犊崮与熊耳山Baodu Mountain & Xiong'er Mountain

抱犊崮是鲁南第一高峰，八路军115师曾在这里建立抗日根据地。山上有一个"抱犊崮崮"——就是那种四面陡峭、山顶平坦的山，中国独有的地貌，枣庄有好多。

熊耳山的"双龙大裂谷"是地震形成的自然奇观，裂谷有一百多米深，两壁几乎垂直，你站在谷底抬头看，天只剩一条缝。谷里还有一线天、飞来石，很值得一看。

实用贴士：抱犊崮门票 100 元；熊耳山门票 60 元，裂谷有点险，穿防滑鞋。
Handy tip:Baodu ticket 100 yuan; Xiong'er 60 yuan — the rift has some peril, wear non-slip shoes.

Baodu is southern Shandong's highest peak, where the Eighth Route Army's 115th Division set up an anti-Japanese base. It's a 'gu' — the unique Chinese landform of steep-sided, flat-topped hills — Zaozhuang has many.

Xiong'er Mountain's 'Two Dragons Great Rift' is an earthquake-made wonder — over a hundred metres deep, walls nearly vertical. Stand at the bottom and look up, the sky is a mere crack. Also has One Line Sky and Flying Stone, worth the climb.

实用贴士：抱犊崮门票 100 元；熊耳山门票 60 元，裂谷有点险，穿防滑鞋。
Handy tip:Baodu ticket 100 yuan; Xiong'er 60 yuan — the rift has some peril, wear non-slip shoes.
08

吃什么What to eat

枣庄的味道在"硬"字里——煤矿工人、铁道游击队，都是咬着牙过日子的人。菜煎饼是街头招牌，比山东煎饼厚，夹着土豆丝、胡萝卜丝、豆腐丝，卷起来比胳膊还粗。

羊肉汤也是枣庄人的命根子，跟徐州的羊汤同源，但更浓。冬天一大早，街边羊汤馆就支起大锅，白汤翻滚，撒香菜，配锅饼，一碗下去能扛到中午。

当地贴士：'老郑家菜煎饼"是台儿庄的老招牌；羊汤馆认准门口挂着羊头的。
Local tip:Old Zheng's vegetable pancake is Taierzhuang's signboard; mutton soup stalls — look for the sheep head hanging outside.

Zaozhuang's flavour lives in 'hard' — miners and guerrillas alike bit their teeth through life. Vegetable pancakes are the street sign, thicker than Shandong's, stuffed with potato, carrot, tofu threads, rolled thicker than your arm.

Mutton soup is Zaozhuang's lifeline, same origin as Xuzhou's but thicker. By early winter mornings, street stalls boil big pots, white broth rolling, sprinkled with coriander, with wheat cakes — one bowl holds you till noon.

当地贴士：'老郑家菜煎饼"是台儿庄的老招牌；羊汤馆认准门口挂着羊头的。
Local tip:Old Zheng's vegetable pancake is Taierzhuang's signboard; mutton soup stalls — look for the sheep head hanging outside.
09

懂点规矩Know the ropes

台儿庄大捷纪念馆是红色圣地，进馆请保持肃穆，请勿喧哗。铁道游击队纪念园同理。古城里的老建筑请勿攀爬。矿区旧路别乱闯，有的还在用。

枣庄人直来直去，说话硬，可对陌生人很热情。问路他们会给你掰扯得明明白白，还可能邀你去家里喝碗羊汤。别对他们的抗日英雄有任何不敬——这座城把那些名字刻在了砖上、墙上、心里。

Taierzhuang memorial is sacred heritage — silence inside, no loud talk. Same for the Railway Guerrilla memorial. Don't climb old structures in the town. Stay off old mine roads — some are still in use.

Zaozhuang folk are straight, speak hard, but warm to strangers. They'll point you clear directions and might even invite you in for mutton soup. Never show disrespect to their anti-Japanese heroes — those names are carved into bricks, walls, and hearts.

10

什么时候去When to go

秋天古城的灯会最漂亮，运河上游船穿梭，灯笼倒映在水里像落了一片星。春天山上的杏花梨花开了，抱犊崮的景色最好。夏天热，但可以在运河上坐船消暑。冬天冷，可旧矿区的烟囱配雪，有一种荒凉的美。

若为了古城灯会，秋天最对；若为了登山赏花，春天最好。

实用贴士：抗战胜利纪念日（九月三号）有纪念活动，人多；避开周末更清静。
Handy tip:Victory Day (September 3rd) has events — expect crowds; weekends are busier too.

Autumn brings the town's loveliest lantern festival — boats shuttle on the canal, lanterns reflecting like a sky of stars. Spring blossoms on the mountains, Baodu at its best. Summer's hot, but a canal boat cools you. Winter's cold, but old mine chimneys against snow have a desolate beauty.

For the lantern festival, autumn fits; for mountain blossoms, spring is best.

实用贴士：抗战胜利纪念日（九月三号）有纪念活动，人多；避开周末更清静。
Handy tip:Victory Day (September 3rd) has events — expect crowds; weekends are busier too.
11

去哪悠走Where to wander

台儿庄古城的运河步道免费，从古城南门能一直走到西门，沿途有古码头和老桥。铁道游击队纪念园的铁轨也免费开放，可以上去走走。抱犊崮的登山步道免费（需先买门票），山上空气好得能当矿泉水卖。

枣庄的好走，在于它"不完美"——旧矿区的废墟、运河边的垃圾堆、古城里还没修完的地方，都有一种真实的味道。

实用贴士：'22 Day"纪念日前古城会有抗战主题活动；旧矿区不推荐单独前往。
Handy tip:Around July 22nd the old town hosts war-themed events; don't go to old mines alone.

The Taierzhuang canal path is free, from south gate to west gate with old quays and bridges. The Railway Guerrilla railway tracks are open free. Baodu's hiking path costs nothing after ticket; mountain air clean enough to sell as water.

Zaozhuang is walkable because it's 'not perfect' — old mine ruins, canal-side garbage, parts of the town still being fixed — all have that honest, real feel.

实用贴士：'22 Day"纪念日前古城会有抗战主题活动；旧矿区不推荐单独前往。
Handy tip:Around July 22nd the old town hosts war-themed events; don't go to old mines alone.
12

不花钱也值得Worth it free

枣庄最贵的东西，大多不要钱。运河的水声、古城的红灯笼、抱犊崮上的风，站着看就是参与。铁道游击队纪念园免费，能让你重新理解那段历史。

枣庄教会我：最动人的风景往往不是完美的，而是带着点伤痕的。

当地贴士：'台儿庄古城外围运河步道免费；铁道游击队纪念园免费。
Local tip:Canal path outside Taierzhuang is free; Railway Guerrilla memorial is free.

Zaozhuang's priciest things mostly cost nothing — the canal's sound, the old town's red lanterns, wind on Baodu Mountain; stand and you're in it. The Railway Guerrilla memorial is free, and will make you rethink that period of history.

Zaozhuang taught me: the most moving landscapes aren't the perfect ones — they're the ones that still carry scars.

13

收束Before you leave

枣庄不会一下子把你看服。它没有名山的名气，也没有水乡的婉约。它硬，它惨，它又韧又燃——一个古城能让人沉默，一段铁轨能让人回头。

你看过台儿庄的灯笼、铁道游击队的墙、抱犊崮的风，临走会明白：淮海不只有汉墓运河，还有一座城在废墟上自己把灯点亮。它不急着留你，却总让你惦记。

Zaozhuang won't win you at once. No famous mountain's fame, no water town's grace. It's hard, tragic, tough and burning — one old town silences you, one rail turns you back.

You've seen Taierzhuang's lanterns, the guerrilla wall, Baodu's wind — and leaving you'll get it: Huaihai isn't only Han tombs and canals; there's a city that lit its own lanterns out of the ruins. It won't rush to keep you, yet always leaves you missing it.
