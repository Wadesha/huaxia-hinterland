01 一座很容易被念错名的城A city whose name is always misread

安徽有个宿州，江苏有个宿迁，两个都在淮海一带，别搞混。

宿州在安徽最北，往北就是江苏，往西是河南。

它 1999 年才撤地设市，城里到处是新修的路。

你在街上看，招牌上最多的是烧鸡和梨。

这两样东西，基本就是这座城的自我介绍。

There is a Suzhou in Anhui and a Suqian in Jiangsu, both in this Huaihai corner, and people mix them up constantly.

Suzhou sits at Anhui's northern edge, with Jiangsu to the north and Henan to the west.

It only became a prefecture-level city in 1999, and the town is full of newly laid roads.

Look at the shopfronts: roast chicken and pears, mostly.

Those two things are more or less how the city introduces itself.

02 符离集：一路烧鸡，一个镇Fuliji: a town that is one long roast chicken

符离集在市区北边，是个镇，也是个火车站名。

镇上的主业是烧鸡，一家挨一家，香味一路飘。

你随便挑一家，鸡是现捞的，撕开就吃。

肉烂，骨头酥，香料味进得深。

高铁上很多人提着真空包装的烧鸡，就是从这带出去的。

Fuliji is a town north of the city, and also a station name.

The town's trade is roast chicken, shop after shop, the smell following you down the street.

Pick any one of them: the bird comes out of the pot and is pulled apart by hand.

The meat falls away, the bones are soft, and the spice has gone in deep.

Half the vacuum-packed chickens people carry onto trains come from around here.

03 皇藏峪：山里藏过一个人Huangcangyu: a hill that hid a man

皇藏峪在萧县，离市区有点远，是一片山峪。

传说刘邦在这儿躲过追兵，所以叫皇藏峪。

峪里树很老，青檀长在石头缝里，根把石头包住了。

你沿着石阶往上走，林子密，光是碎的。

山顶有座寺，香火不旺，但安静。

Huangcangyu is in Xiaoxian county, some way from the city — a wooded valley.

The story goes that Liu Bang hid here from his pursuers, which is how the valley got its name: the hiding place of an emperor.

The trees are ancient; the wingceltis grows straight out of rock, its roots wrapping the stone.

You climb stone steps through dense woodland, the light broken into pieces.

There is a temple at the top, not much visited and very quiet.

04 灵璧石：石头也有长相Lingbi stone: stones with faces

灵璧在宿州东南，出一种石头，是全国最有名的观赏石之一。

灵璧石黑，敲起来有声音，讲究皱、瘦、漏、透。

你在市场上逛，一块石头可以卖几千，也可以卖几十万。

外行看热闹，看形状像什么，看表面的纹路。

真要买，先多问几家，价钱差得没边。

Lingbi, southeast of Suzhou, produces a stone that is among the best-known scholar's rocks in China.

Lingbi stone is black, rings when struck, and is judged on wrinkle, thinness, perforation and translucency.

Walk the market: one stone goes for a few thousand, another for hundreds of thousands.

Outsiders look for the fun of it — what the shape resembles, how the surface is veined.

If you mean to buy, ask at several stalls first; the spread in price has no floor.

05 砀山：春天一整片白Dangshan: a whole white spring

砀山在宿州最北边，靠着黄河故道。

这儿是产梨的地方，梨园一片接一片。

春天开花的时候，地里全是白的，风一吹落一层。

你走进梨园，脚下是沙土，头顶是花。

秋天结果，砀山酥梨皮薄汁多，到处都在卖。

Dangshan is at Suzhou's northern edge, along the old bed of the Yellow River.

This is pear country, orchard after orchard.

When it flowers in spring the whole ground turns white, and the wind brings down a layer of petals.

Walk into the orchards: sandy soil underfoot, blossom overhead.

Autumn brings the fruit — Dangshan pears, thin-skinned and juicy, on sale everywhere.

06 大泽乡：一句喊出来的话Dazexiang: one shouted sentence

涉故台在埇桥区的大泽乡，是一处土台。

公元前 209 年，陈胜吴广在这儿揭竿而起。

你站在台前，四周是田，什么也没有。

"王侯将相宁有种乎"这句话，就是从这附近喊出去的。

台不大，土是夯的，边上立着碑。

Shegudai is a platform of rammed earth at Dazexiang in Yongqiao district.

In 209 BC Chen Sheng and Wu Guang raised their revolt on this spot.

Stand in front of the platform: fields on every side and nothing else.

"Are kings and generals born to the purple?" — that line went out from somewhere around here.

The platform is small, made of tamped earth, with a stele beside it.

07 虞姬墓The tomb of Consort Yu

灵璧有座虞姬墓，说是项羽那位虞姬的墓。

墓不大，园子修得规整，碑是新的。

你绕着走一圈，几分钟就完了。

故事的分量比坟本身重——垓下那夜，她死在霸王前面。

本地人把这当作一段旧事，不悲不喜。

Lingbi has a tomb said to be that of Consort Yu, Xiang Yu's woman.

The tomb is small, the grounds tidy, the stele new.

Walk around it and you are done in a few minutes.

The story outweighs the grave — that night at Gaixia, she died before the hegemon did.

Locals treat it as an old story, without either grief or pride.

08 运河从地下过The canal runs underneath

隋唐大运河从宿州城里穿过，如今埋在地下。

前些年挖出来一段河道和码头，建了遗址展示。

你在市区就能走到，不用专门跑远路。

展馆不大，看地层剖面最直观，一层一层都是年代。

出了馆是马路和小区，运河在哪儿只能靠牌子认。

The Sui–Tang Grand Canal once ran through Suzhou, and now lies underground.

Some years ago a stretch of channel and a quay were excavated and opened as a heritage site.

It is within the city, so no special trip is needed.

The museum is small; the stratigraphic section is the clearest thing in it, layer upon layer of periods.

Outside are roads and housing estates, and only a sign tells you where the canal ran.

09 吃：鸡、馍与汤Food: chicken, bread and soup

宿州的吃很北方，量大，油重，价低。

符离集烧鸡是头牌，热的最好吃，凉的也不差。

街上有一种烙馍，卷菜卷肉，一张顶一顿。

你早上去喝汤，配馍，几块钱就饱了。

这里不太讲究摆盘，好吃管饱就算合格。

Suzhou food is northern: large portions, plenty of oil, low prices.

Fuliji roast chicken is the headline dish, best hot, still good cold.

There is a griddle bread on the streets, rolled around vegetables or meat, one sheet to a meal.

Go for soup in the early hours, bread alongside it, and a few yuan fills you up.

Presentation is not a priority here; filling and tasting good is enough to pass.

实用贴士：符离集烧鸡买热的现吃，带走的真空装口味差一截；砀山梨以秋季为佳，春天去主要看花，花期通常只有十来天。
Tip:Buy Fuliji chicken hot and eat it there — the vacuum-packed version loses a step. Dangshan pears are best in autumn; a spring visit is about the blossom, which usually lasts little more than ten days.
10 几件要紧事A few things that matter

宿州的看点分散在几个县：砀山在最北，萧县在西，灵璧在东南。

你按县走，一天跑一个地方比较稳。

高铁站是宿州东站，离市区有段路。

各县之间有班车，班次不算密，赶时间就打车或包车。

春天看花，秋天吃梨，别的时候来就专心吃鸡。

Suzhou's sights scatter across its counties: Dangshan in the far north, Xiaoxian west, Lingbi southeast.

Take them county by county — one place a day is a realistic pace.

The high-speed station is Suzhou East, some way out of town.

Coaches run between the counties but not often, so hire a car or take a taxi if time is short.

Come in spring for blossom, autumn for pears, and at any other time simply for the chicken.

实用贴士：砀山、萧县、灵璧三处方向各不相同，与市区车程均在一小时上下；皇藏峪在萧县境内，进峪后全程步行，来回约 3 小时。
Tip:Dangshan, Xiaoxian and Lingbi lie in three different directions, each about an hour from the city. Huangcangyu is inside Xiaoxian, and the valley is walked end to end — around three hours there and back.
11 离开的时候On leaving

宿州不怎么上榜单，也没什么必看的东西。

可你吃了鸡，看了梨花，摸过那些黑石头，会觉得不虚此行。

这地方的东西都很具体：一只鸡、一块石、一片梨园、一个土台。

没有一样是宏大的，凑起来却挺像回事。

走的时候你会提一只烧鸡上车。

Suzhou does not appear on many lists, and it has nothing you must see.

But you eat the chicken, see the blossom, handle the black stones, and the trip does not feel wasted.

Everything here is concrete: a chicken, a stone, an orchard, a mound of earth.

Not one of them is grand, and together they amount to something.

You leave carrying a roast chicken onto the train.

01 先分清两个宿Telling the two Sus apart

安徽的宿州与江苏的宿迁同属淮海一带，名字相近而毫不相干，外地人几乎每次都要弄错一次。

宿州位于安徽最北端，北接江苏徐州，西邻河南，是典型的省际交界城市，1999 年才撤地设市。

境内以平原为主，黄河故道横贯砀山、萧县一线，留下的沙质土壤后来成了大片果园的基础。

市区本身很新，看点几乎都在下辖的几个县里，进城只是中转，真正的目的地分散在砀山、萧县与灵璧。

Suzhou in Anhui and Suqian in Jiangsu both belong to this Huaihai corner; the names are close and the places have nothing to do with each other, so nearly every visitor gets it wrong once.

Suzhou sits at Anhui's northern tip, meeting Xuzhou in Jiangsu to the north and Henan to the west — a border city in the classic sense, and only raised to prefecture-level status in 1999.

The land is mostly plain; the old Yellow River bed runs across Dangshan and Xiaoxian, and the sandy soil it left became the basis for extensive orchards.

The city itself is new and almost everything worth seeing lies in the counties it administers: the town is a transfer point, and the actual destinations are scattered through Dangshan, Xiaoxian and Lingbi.

02 符离集：一个以鸡为业的小镇Fuliji: a town that lives on chicken

市区以北的符离集既是镇名也是车站名，镇上的主业高度集中在一种食物上：烧鸡。

做法是先卤后炸或者先炸后卤，各家配方不同，共同点是香料下得重，出锅时肉烂骨酥，用手就能撕开。

沿街的店铺一家挨一家，价格相差不大，本地人各有自己认准的那一家，彼此不太说服得动。

热食与真空包装是两回事，现出锅的口感与带走的那一种差着明显的距离，值得在镇上吃一次。

Fuliji, north of the city, is both a town and a station name, and the town's trade is concentrated almost entirely on one food: roast chicken.

The method is either braised-then-fried or fried-then-braised, and every shop has its own recipe; what they share is a heavy hand with spice and a bird that comes out so soft it can be pulled apart by hand.

Shop after shop lines the street, prices differ little, and every local has a house they swear by, none of them persuadable.

Eating it there and carrying it away are two different things — the bird fresh from the pot and the vacuum-packed version are clearly not the same, and it is worth eating once in the town.

03 皇藏峪：一片藏过皇帝的山峪Huangcangyu: the valley that hid an emperor

萧县境内的皇藏峪是一处石灰岩山地，相传刘邦在被追兵围困时藏身于此，"皇藏"二字由此而来。

峪中林木茂密，青檀是主要的古树种类，根系沿岩缝生长，常常把整块石头包住，形成根石相缠的形态。

石阶一路向上，林中光线碎而不暗，夏季峪内温度明显低于外面，是这一带少有的避暑地面。

山顶有瑞云寺，规模不大，香火清淡；你要是想躲开人群，这里的安静程度超出预期。

Huangcangyu in Xiaoxian is a limestone valley where Liu Bang is said to have hidden from his pursuers — the name means, roughly, the hiding place of an emperor.

The valley is densely wooded, wingceltis being the principal old tree; its roots run along fissures in the rock and often close over whole boulders, producing the entwined root-and-stone forms the place is known for.

Stone steps climb throughout, the light in the woods broken but not dim, and in summer the valley runs noticeably cooler than the land outside — rare relief in this part of the plain.

On the summit stands Ruiyun temple, small and lightly visited; if you want to be away from a crowd, the quiet here exceeds expectation.

04 灵璧石：被看了一千年的石头Lingbi stone: stones studied for a millennium

灵璧县出产的灵璧石是中国传统观赏石的主要品种之一，色黑如漆，叩之有声，宋人已经把它列入文房清供。

评鉴的标准沿用至今：皱、瘦、漏、透四条，讲究表面纹理、体态、孔洞与层次，一块石头也可以因声音单独成立。

市场里的差价极大，同一方石头在不同摊位之间可以相差数倍，外行看形状与纹理即可，出手前多问几家。

这门审美在今天并不主流，但它把"石头该长什么样"这个问题讨论了一千年，现场看看那些纹路，比读解释更快懂。

Lingbi stone from Lingbi county is one of the principal varieties of traditional Chinese scholar's rock: black as lacquer, resonant when struck, and already listed among the refinements of the scholar's desk in the Song.

The criteria have not changed: wrinkle, thinness, perforation and translucency, judging surface texture, posture, hollows and strata, with some pieces valued for sound alone.

Prices in the market vary enormously, and the same stone can be quoted several times over between stalls; an outsider can look at shape and texture, and should ask at several before buying.

This aesthetic is not mainstream today, but it spent a thousand years discussing what a stone ought to look like — look at the veining on site and you understand it faster than from any explanation.

05 砀山：黄河故道上的果园Dangshan: orchards on the old river bed

砀山位于宿州最北，正处在黄河故道上，1855 年黄河改道之后留下的沙质土层，透气性好而肥力不高，恰好适合梨树。

这里的梨园以规模取胜，砀山酥梨栽培历史悠久，果皮薄、汁多、渣少，秋季上市时公路两侧皆是收购与批发的摊点。

春季的花期是另一回事：满地梨花同时开放，一片接一片，花期通常只有十来天，来得早或晚都白跑。

沙土、白花与秋梨这三件事其实是同一件事——一条改道而去的河，把这里的产业定了下来。

Dangshan lies at Suzhou's northern edge, directly on the old Yellow River bed; the sandy soil left behind after the river changed course in 1855 drains well and is not rich, which suits pear trees exactly.

The orchards win on scale: Dangshan crisp pears have been grown here for centuries, thin-skinned, juicy and nearly without grit, and in autumn the roadsides fill with buying and wholesale points.

The spring flowering is another matter entirely: pear blossom opens across the whole ground at once, orchard after orchard, for a window usually no longer than ten days — arrive early or late and you have come for nothing.

Sand, white blossom and autumn pears are in the end one story: a river that changed course decided what this place would grow.

06 大泽乡与涉故台Dazexiang and Shegudai

埇桥区大泽乡的涉故台是一处夯土台址，公元前 209 年，陈胜、吴广在此率戍卒起事，被认为是中国历史上第一次大规模农民起义的起点。

起义只持续了数月便告失败，但它留下的那句"王侯将相宁有种乎"，把血统与权力之间的关系第一次直接摆上了台面。

现场能看到的很少：一座土台、几块碑、四周的农田，规模与它在中国史书里的分量完全不成比例。

也正因为空，站在这里反而更容易想象——一支被大雨困住、误了期限的队伍，在一片平原上做出决定。

Shegudai at Dazexiang in Yongqiao district is a platform of rammed earth; in 209 BC Chen Sheng and Wu Guang led the conscripts rising here, generally taken as the starting point of the first large-scale peasant uprising in Chinese history.

The revolt failed within a few months, but the line it left behind — are kings, nobles, generals and ministers born to the purple? — put the relationship between blood and power on the table directly for the first time.

There is very little to see: a mound of earth, a few steles, farmland on all sides, a scale entirely out of proportion to the weight it carries in the histories.

And precisely because it is empty, it is easier to picture: a detachment held up by rain, past its deadline, making a decision out on a plain.

07 虞姬墓The tomb of Consort Yu

灵璧县境内的虞姬墓，相传是项羽爱妾虞姬的埋骨之处，垓下之战的那个夜晚，她先于霸王自尽。

现存墓冢并不大，园子修得规整，碑为后世所立，可看的东西几分钟就能走完一圈。

它的价值不在实物，而在于这一段故事在中国的戏曲、戏曲唱词与评书里被反复讲述了两千多年。

本地人对此态度平淡，既不当成荣耀也不当成悲情；你若读过垓下那段，站在这儿的感觉会不一样。

The tomb of Consort Yu in Lingbi is said to be where Xiang Yu's beloved was buried — the woman who took her own life before the hegemon did, on the night of the battle of Gaixia.

The surviving mound is small, the grounds tidy, the stele a later erection, and a circuit of it takes only a few minutes.

Its value is not in the object but in the story, which opera, ballad and storytelling have retold in China for more than two thousand years.

Locals are matter-of-fact about it, treating it as neither honour nor tragedy; if you know that night at Gaixia, standing here feels different.

08 运河：一段埋在城底的隋唐The canal: Sui–Tang beneath the streets

隋唐大运河的通济渠段曾穿过今宿州市区，河道淤废之后被城市层层覆盖，二十一世纪初的考古工作重新找到了它的位置。

发掘出的河道、码头与大量瓷片，为运河的走向与当时的运输规模提供了直接证据，如今以遗址展示的方式留在市区。

展馆不大，最直观的是地层剖面：不同年代的文化层一层压一层，河道的形状在剖面上清清楚楚。

出馆即是马路与住宅小区，河在哪里只剩一块牌子说明；这种"就在脚下却看不见"的落差，是遗址类景点最特别的地方。

The Tongji section of the Sui–Tang Grand Canal once crossed what is now central Suzhou; after it silted up, the city buried it layer by layer, and archaeological work in the early 2000s found its line again.

The channel, a quay and large quantities of sherds that came out of the ground gave direct evidence for the canal's course and for the scale of transport on it, and they are now kept as a heritage site inside the city.

The museum is small, and the clearest object in it is the stratigraphic section: occupation layers of different periods stacked one on another, the shape of the channel legible in the cut.

Step outside and there are roads and housing estates, with only a board to say where the water was — the gap between "under your feet" and "nowhere to be seen" is the peculiar thing about sites like this.

09 吃：鸡、馍与汤Food: chicken, bread and soup

宿州的饮食结构偏北，面食为主，油量偏重，份量与价格之间的关系对食客相当友好。

符离集烧鸡是这里的头牌，热食最佳，撕开后肉与骨自然分离，冷食与真空包装都各自差着一档。

街头常见的是烙馍，一张薄饼卷菜卷肉，既是主食也是容器，一张下去基本顶一顿。

早间的汤馆是最能看清本地口味的地方：汤浓、胡椒重、配馍，几块钱吃完，一天开始。

The food here is northern in structure — wheat-based, generous with oil, and the ratio of portion to price is very much in the eater's favour.

Fuliji roast chicken is the headline, best eaten hot, the meat separating from the bone as you pull it; cold or vacuum-packed, it is a step down each time.

What you see on the street is griddle bread, a thin sheet rolled around vegetables or meat, staple and container at once, one of them generally making a meal.

The soup houses of the early hours show the local palate most clearly: thick soup, plenty of pepper, bread alongside, a few yuan, and the day begins.

实用贴士：符离集烧鸡买热的现吃，带走的真空装口味差一截；砀山梨以秋季为佳，春天去主要看花，花期通常只有十来天。
Tip:Buy Fuliji chicken hot and eat it there — the vacuum-packed version loses a step. Dangshan pears are best in autumn; a spring visit is about the blossom, which usually lasts little more than ten days.
10 方向与距离Directions and distances

宿州的看点分散在砀山、萧县与灵璧三个县，方向各不相同，与市区的车程都在一小时上下，一天跑一处比较稳。

高铁停靠宿州东站，离市区有一段距离；各县之间有班车，但班次不密，赶时间更适合打车或包车。

砀山看花在春季、吃梨在秋季，两个季节之外来，主要目标应当放在吃与皇藏峪的林子上。

皇藏峪进峪后全程步行，来回约 3 小时，路面湿滑的雨后要格外小心。

Suzhou's sights are spread across Dangshan, Xiaoxian and Lingbi, three counties in three directions, each about an hour from the city — one place a day is a realistic pace.

High-speed trains stop at Suzhou East, some way from the centre; coaches run between the counties but not often, so a taxi or a hired car suits anyone short of time.

Dangshan is for blossom in spring and pears in autumn; outside those two seasons, the reasons to come are the food and the woods at Huangcangyu.

Huangcangyu is walked end to end, around three hours there and back, and the paths need real care after rain.

实用贴士：砀山、萧县、灵璧三处方向各不相同，与市区车程均在一小时上下；皇藏峪在萧县境内，进峪后全程步行，来回约 3 小时，雨后路滑。
Tip:Dangshan, Xiaoxian and Lingbi lie in three different directions, each about an hour from the city. Huangcangyu is inside Xiaoxian, walked end to end in about three hours, and slippery after rain.
11 具体事物的城A city of concrete things

宿州很少出现在任何榜单上，也没有哪一处是"非看不可"的，这一点它自己大概也承认。

但它的东西都很具体：一只卤透的鸡、一方被摩挲千年的黑石、一片同时开花的梨园、一座夯土的台。

没有一样宏大，凑在一起却构成了一个完整的剖面——起义、帝王、文人审美、果木生计，各占一层。

它不解释自己。看得懂看不懂，终究是你自己的事。

Suzhou appears on few lists and holds nothing you could call unmissable, a fact it would probably concede itself.

But everything it has is concrete: a thoroughly braised chicken, a black stone handled for a thousand years, an orchard that flowers all at once, a mound of tamped earth.

Not one of them is grand, yet together they form a complete section — uprising, emperor, scholar's taste, fruit-growing livelihood, each in its own layer.

It does not explain itself. Whether you read it or not is, in the end, your own business.
