01 先说清楚这是哪First, where this is

宿迁是江苏最年轻的地级市之一，1996 年才从老淮阴分出来。

你在地图上看，它夹在徐州、连云港和淮安之间。

城里到处是新的路和新的楼，招牌上写着"项王故里""中国酒都"。

别被这些新东西骗了，这地方的老底子比你想的深。

你先去老城区走一圈，节奏会慢下来。

Suqian is one of Jiangsu's youngest prefecture-level cities, split off from the old Huaiyin prefecture only in 1996.

On the map it is wedged between Xuzhou, Lianyungang and Huai'an.

The town is full of new roads and new blocks, with signs reading "home of King Xiang" and "China's liquor capital".

Don't be fooled by the newness — what lies underneath is older than you would guess.

Walk the old centre first and the pace drops.

02 项王故里：一个败者的老家Home of a king who lost

项羽出生在下相，也就是今天的宿迁。

项王故里的院子是后来修的，格局规整，柏树很老。

院里有一棵老槐，相传是他亲手栽的，树干粗得要两人合抱。

你站在树下会想，这个人打到最后一刻也不肯过江。

本地人讲他，语气里没有成败，只有熟。

Xiang Yu was born in Xiaxiang, which is Suqian today.

The compound at Xiang Yu's old home is a later rebuild, formally laid out, with very old cypresses.

In the courtyard stands an ancient locust tree said to have been planted by his own hand, its trunk too thick for one pair of arms.

Standing under it, you think of a man who fought to the last and refused to cross the river.

Locals speak of him without any sense of winning or losing, only familiarity.

03 骆马湖：一片大水Luoma Lake: a wide water

骆马湖在城北，湖面很大，风一吹就起浪。

湖边有湿地公园，芦苇长得比人高。

你可以沿湖骑车，路平，人少，鸟多。

渔民还在湖上干活，船很小，网很大。

天好的时候，落日把整片水面压成一条金线。

Luoma Lake lies north of the city, a wide sheet of water that lifts into waves as soon as the wind gets up.

There is a wetland park along the shore where the reeds grow taller than a person.

You can cycle the lake road: flat, almost empty, full of birds.

Fishermen still work the lake in small boats with very large nets.

On a clear day the low sun presses the whole surface down into a single gold line.

04 皂河：一座给龙王盖的行宫Zaohe: a palace built for a river god

皂河镇在城西北，镇上有座很大的庙。

庙是给龙王盖的，后来乾隆南巡住过，所以也叫行宫。

你走进去，第一进是庙，后面几进是宫殿格局，规格高得反常。

梁上的彩画旧了，颜色还在。

这种"庙里套宫殿"的怪样子，全国都不多见。

Zaohe town is northwest of the city, and it has a very large temple.

It was built for the dragon king, and the Qianlong emperor stayed here on his southern tours, which is why it is also called a travelling palace.

Go in and the first hall is a temple, while the halls behind are laid out as a palace — an oddly high specification.

The painted beams have faded but the colour holds.

A palace tucked inside a temple is not something you see often anywhere in the country.

05 酒：这座城的另一半Liquor: the other half of the city

宿迁有两个名酒：洋河和双沟，一个在城南，一个在泗洪。

酒厂很大，厂区里有自己的路和树。

你参观的时候先闻到味，然后才看见窖池。

老窖池的泥是黑的，据说几十年没换过。

本地人喝酒不看牌子，看谁家当年出的货好。

Suqian has two famous liquors: Yanghe and Shuanggou, one south of the city, one in Sihong.

The distilleries are enormous, with their own roads and their own trees inside the gates.

On a visit you smell it first, and only then see the fermentation pits.

The mud in the old pits is black, and they say it has not been changed in decades.

Locals do not drink by label; they drink by which year's batch turned out well.

06 三台山与衲田Three Terrace Mountain and the patchwork fields

三台山在城北，不高，林子密。

山下的衲田是一片拼起来的花田，一块一块，颜色不同。

你沿着田埂走，两边是花，远处是水。

花开的时候人多，不开的时候也照样好看，看的是田的形状。

这里适合慢慢走，不适合赶。

Three Terrace Mountain is north of the city, low and thickly wooded.

Below it the patchwork fields are a mosaic of flower plots, block by block, each a different colour.

You walk the earth ridges with flowers on both sides and water in the distance.

When the blooms come the crowds come too, but it looks just as good without them — it is the shape of the fields you are looking at.

This is a place for walking slowly, not for hurrying.

07 下草湾：几万年前的人Xiacaowan: people from tens of thousands of years ago

泗洪有处下草湾遗址，几十年前出土过古人类的骨头。

化石不多，说法不少，年代以万年计。

现场没什么可看的，就是一片草地和一个说明牌。

你站在那儿，脚下是几万年前有人活动过的土层。

这种地方的价值不在眼睛，在时间。

At Sihong there is the Xiacaowan site, where bones of early humans were dug up some decades ago.

The fossils are few and the theories many, and the date runs in tens of thousands of years.

There is little to see on site: a patch of grass and an explanatory board.

Stand there and the soil under your feet is ground where people were active tens of thousands of years ago.

The value of a place like this is not in what you see, but in how deep the time runs.

08 运河从城边过The canal passes the edge of town

京杭大运河从宿迁穿过去，水是活的，船是动的。

你能看见拖船一长串地走，一艘拖着十几艘。

河边修了步道，入夜有人散步，也有人在钓鱼。

这条河把这座城和更南更北的地方连在一起。

它老得不能再老了，却还在干活。

The Grand Canal runs through Suqian, the water alive and the boats moving.

You see tug trains passing in long strings, one boat pulling a dozen behind it.

A path has been built along the bank; people walk it and people fish from it.

The river ties this city to places much further south and much further north.

It is as old as anything can be, and it is still working.

09 吃：烧鸡、糁汤与面Chicken, soup and noodles

宿迁的吃偏北方，面食多，口味重。

符离集烧鸡的名气在隔壁安徽，可这边也吃，肉烂骨酥。

糁汤是过早的东西，胡椒重，配馍或者油条。

你要是能吃辣，就尝尝小鱼熬辣椒，很下饭。

价钱都不高，一碗一盘，吃得人发困。

Suqian food leans north: plenty of wheat, and flavours that do not hold back.

Fuliji roast chicken is famous over the border in Anhui, but it is eaten here too, the meat falling off the bone.

Sa tang is a breakfast thing, heavy with pepper, taken with bread or fried dough.

If you can take chilli, try small fish stewed with it — it goes a long way with rice.

None of it costs much; a bowl and a plate and you are ready for a nap.

实用贴士：洋河酒厂的参观通常要走预约流程，散客先打听当日是否开放；酒都的餐馆入夜生意最好，去晚了要等位。
Tip:Visits to the Yanghe distillery generally run on an appointment system, so ask whether it is open to individuals that day. Restaurants here do their best trade at night, and late means queuing.
10 几件要紧事A few things that matter

宿迁的景点分得散，湖在北，酒厂在南，项王故里在老城。

你按方向排，别来回跑。

高铁站是宿迁站，离市区有段路，打车最省事。

城里公交便宜，但收班早，别玩到太晚。

湖边和山上风大，春天和秋天最舒服。

Suqian's sights point different ways: the lake north, the distilleries south, Xiang Yu's old home in the old town.

Group them by direction; don't keep doubling back.

The high-speed station is Suqian, some way out of town, and a taxi is the least trouble.

Buses in the city are cheap but stop running early, so don't stay out too late.

It is windy by the lake and on the hills; spring and autumn are the comfortable seasons.

实用贴士：项王故里、皂河龙王庙、三台山分别位于老城、西北与城北，公交可达但耗时；骆马湖环湖路适合骑行，沿途补给点少，出发前带水。
Tip:Xiang Yu's old home, the Zaohe dragon-king temple and Three Terrace Mountain sit in the old town, the northwest and the north respectively — reachable by bus but slow. The loop road around Luoma Lake suits cycling, though supply points are few, so carry water.
11 离开的时候On leaving

宿迁不热闹，也不难看。

它是那种你路过时会想"原来这儿也有东西"的城。

一个败了的霸王，一湖水，几口老窖，一条还在跑船的运河。

这些东西凑在一起，不响，但压得住。

你走的时候大概不会拍照，但会记得味道。

Suqian is not lively, and it is not ugly either.

It is the kind of city where you pass through and think: so there is something here after all.

A king who lost, a lake, a few old fermentation pits, and a canal still carrying boats.

Put together they make no noise, but they hold their ground.

You probably won't take photographs on the way out, but you will remember the smell.

01 一座很新的城，压着很旧的底A new city over old ground

宿迁在 1996 年从老淮阴地区分出，成为江苏较年轻的地级市，城市面貌因此几乎全是新的：宽路、新楼、平整的行政区块。

它夹在徐州、连云港与淮安之间，方言、面食与口味都偏北，与苏南那种精致的调子相去甚远。

但新只是表层：往地下数，有数万年前人类活动过的土层；往历史里数，有楚汉相争与明清漕运。

新的路牌上写着"项王故里"与"中国酒都"，这两块招牌都不是新造的词，一个两千多年，一个也有几十年。

Suqian was carved out of the old Huaiyin prefecture in 1996, making it one of Jiangsu's younger prefecture-level cities, and almost everything visible is accordingly new: wide roads, new blocks, tidily laid-out administrative districts.

It sits wedged between Xuzhou, Lianyungang and Huai'an, and its dialect, its wheat-based food and its tastes all lean north, a long way from the refined register of southern Jiangsu.

But newness is only the surface: below ground lies soil where people were active tens of thousands of years ago, and in the record lie the Chu–Han wars and the grain transport of the Ming and Qing.

The new signs read "home of King Xiang" and "China's liquor capital" — neither term was invented recently; one is more than two thousand years old, the other several decades.

02 项王故里：为一位败者留的院子A courtyard kept for the man who lost

项羽生于下相，即今天的宿迁，时间一般记为公元前 232 年；他在此度过少年，此后起兵、破秦、封王，最终在垓下自刎。

今存的项王故里是历代重修的院落，中轴对称，古柏数株，其中相传为项羽手植的槐树是整座院子最被看重的一棵树。

院子不大，看点也不在规模，而在于中国人为一位失败者保存故里的习惯——成王败寇的说法在这里并不适用。

本地人谈起他时语气平淡，既不惋惜也不夸耀，像说一个很早就出门、再没回来的同乡。

Xiang Yu was born at Xiaxiang — modern Suqian — in what is usually given as 232 BC; he spent his boyhood here before raising an army, breaking Qin, taking a kingdom, and finally cutting his own throat at Gaixia.

The compound that stands today is the product of repeated rebuilding over the centuries: a symmetrical courtyard, several ancient cypresses, and the locust tree said to have been planted by Xiang Yu himself, the most regarded tree in the grounds.

The compound is not large and the point is not scale, but rather the Chinese habit of keeping a home for the man who lost — the saying about the winner becoming king does not apply here.

Locals speak of him flatly, neither mourning nor boasting, as though describing a fellow townsman who left early and never came back.

03 骆马湖Luoma Lake

骆马湖位于城北，是江苏省的主要淡水湖之一，湖面开阔，稍有风力便起浪，岸线以浅滩与芦苇为主。

沿湖修了湿地公园与骑行绿道，芦苇高过人头，鸟类种类不少，观鸟的人多在此停留。

湖上的渔业并未停歇，小木船与大拖网仍在作业，岸边常有刚起的水产直接过秤。

日落时分光线压得很低，整个湖面被收成一条窄而亮的线，是这片水最值得等的一刻。

Luoma Lake lies north of the city and is one of Jiangsu's principal freshwater lakes: broad, quick to raise a chop in any wind, its shoreline mostly shallows and reed beds.

A wetland park and a cycling greenway run along the shore; the reeds stand taller than a person, the birdlife is varied, and birders tend to stop here.

Fishing on the lake has not stopped — small wooden boats and large drag nets still work it, and at the shore the catch is often weighed straight off the water.

At sunset the light comes in very low and the whole surface narrows into a single bright line, the one moment on this water worth waiting for.

04 皂河：庙里的行宫Zaohe: a palace inside a temple

城西北的皂河镇上有一组大型建筑群，前为敕建的安澜龙王庙，后为接待皇帝的宫殿格局，因而常被称作乾隆行宫。

清帝南巡期间多次在此驻跸，这组建筑因此兼有祭祀河神与接驾两套功能，规格之高在一座镇上显得反常。

庙与宫叠在同一条轴线上，这种组合在全国留存不多，也是它最有价值的地方。

梁枋上的彩画已经褪色，颜色仍在；院里石碑的字口也还清楚，能看出当年立碑的人是谁。

At Zaohe, northwest of the city, stands a large complex: an imperially commissioned temple to the dragon king at the front, palace layout behind it to receive the emperor, which is why it is often called the Qianlong travelling palace.

The Qing emperors halted here repeatedly during their southern tours, so the buildings serve two purposes at once — sacrificing to the river god and receiving the sovereign — and the specification is strangely grand for a market town.

Temple and palace stacked on a single axis is a combination that survives in few places in the country, and it is the most valuable thing about the site.

The painted beams have faded but the pigment holds, and the characters cut into the courtyard steles are still legible enough to show who raised them.

05 酒：洋河与双沟Liquor: Yanghe and Shuanggou

宿迁境内有两大白酒产区，城南的洋河与泗洪的双沟，两者都属浓香型，也都在中国名酒之列。

发酵用的老窖池是酒厂真正的核心，窖泥经年累月不换，其中的微生物群落决定了一池酒的脾气。

进了厂区先闻到的是味道，其次才看到窖池、甑桶与酒库，这种顺序在任何一家老酒厂都一样。

本地人买酒不看包装，只认年份与批次，同一品牌不同年份的价差可以很大。

Suqian holds two major baijiu districts, Yanghe south of the city and Shuanggou in Sihong; both make the strong-aroma style, and both are counted among China's famous liquors.

The real core of a distillery is its old fermentation pits: the pit mud is never replaced, and the microbial community living in it decides the character of everything that pit produces.

Enter any old distillery and the smell arrives before the pits, the stills and the warehouses do — the order is the same everywhere.

Locals buy by vintage and batch rather than packaging, and the price gap between two years of the same brand can be wide.

06 三台山与衲田Three Terrace Mountain and the patchwork fields

城北的三台山并不高，山林覆盖良好，山下则是一片按地形拼合的花田，被称作衲田，取其如僧衣百衲之意。

花田按色块分割，田埂纵横，观赏的重点是图案与等高线本身，而不只是花期。

花期人多，非花期安静，两种时候各有各的看法；你若只想走一走，非花期更舒服。

从田里抬头能望见骆马湖的方向，山、田、水在同一个视野里叠起来，是这一带少见的开阔地。

Three Terrace Mountain north of the city is not high and is well wooded; below it lies a flower field pieced together to follow the terrain, called the patchwork field, after the patched robe of a monk.

The field is divided into colour blocks with paths crossing it in both directions, and what is worth seeing is the pattern and the contour lines, not only the bloom.

The flowering season brings crowds, while the off-season brings quiet, and each has its own argument; if you only want a walk, the off-season is kinder.

Looking up from the fields you can see towards Luoma Lake, and hill, field and water stack into a single view — an openness rare in this part of the plain.

07 下草湾：以万年为单位Xiacaowan: counted in tens of thousands of years

泗洪的下草湾遗址在二十世纪中叶出土过古人类化石，年代以数万年计，是这一带最早的人类活动证据之一。

出土材料有限，学界的断代与归属至今仍有讨论，现场能看到的东西也确实不多：一片草地与一块说明牌。

但对一座 1996 年才设市的城市来说，这块牌子意义不小——它把此地的时间轴往前拉了几万倍。

站在那儿，脚下就是当年有人生活过的土层，这类景点的价值从来不在眼睛能收到的那部分。

The Xiacaowan site in Sihong produced early human fossils in the middle of the last century, dated in the tens of thousands of years and among the earliest evidence of human activity in this region.

The material recovered is limited, its dating and classification are still discussed, and there is genuinely little to see: a patch of grass and an explanatory board.

For a city established only in 1996, though, the board carries weight — it stretches the local timeline back tens of thousands of times over.

Stand there and the soil underfoot is ground where people once lived; the value of a site like this has never lain in what the eye receives.

08 运河还在跑船The canal still carries boats

京杭大运河自宿迁境内穿过，河面开阔，驳船以拖带的方式成列行进，一艘动力船后面往往挂着十余艘。

这条人工水道开凿于隋代、定型于元代，至今仍承担货运，是少数还在按原用途运转的古代大型工程。

沿岸修了步道，散步的人与钓鱼的人共用同一段栏杆，货船就在他们脚下过去。

它老得不能再老，却并未变成展品，这种"还在干活"的状态比河本身更值得看。

The Grand Canal crosses Suqian, broad here, with barges moving in towed strings — a dozen or more often hung behind a single powered boat.

Cut in the Sui and set in its present line under the Yuan, this artificial waterway still carries freight: one of the few large ancient engineering works operating at its original purpose.

A path runs along the bank, and walkers and anglers share the same stretch of railing while the cargo boats pass beneath them.

It is as old as anything can be, and yet it has not become an exhibit — that it is still working is more worth seeing than the river itself.

09 吃：面、鸡与小鱼Food: wheat, chicken and small fish

宿迁的饮食偏北方，面食占比高，口味重油重盐，与苏南的清淡形成分明的对照。

符离集烧鸡的名头属安徽宿州，但在此地同样常见，特点是肉烂、骨酥、香料味透进骨头里。

糁汤是早间的常见食物，以禽汤加麦仁与胡椒熬成，辛香浓稠，配馍或油条吃。

小鱼熬辣椒是本地的下饭菜，河湖里的小鱼不去骨，与辣椒同熬，鲜与辣都不含糊。

The food here leans north: wheat dominates, the cooking is heavy on oil and salt, and the contrast with the lightness of southern Jiangsu is sharp.

Fuliji roast chicken carries the name of Suzhou in Anhui, but it is just as common here — meat falling apart, bones softened, spice driven through to the bone.

Sa tang is the usual breakfast dish, simmered from poultry stock with wheat berries and pepper until hot, thick and aromatic, eaten with bread or fried dough.

Small fish stewed with chilli is the local rice-opener: fish from the lakes left unboned and stewed hard with chilli, neither the freshness nor the heat held back.

Tip:Visits to the Yanghe distillery usually need booking in advance, so ask whether it is open to individuals that day. Restaurants do their best trade at night, and late means queuing.
10 方向与距离Directions and distances

宿迁的看点分散在三个方向：骆马湖与三台山在北，洋河与双沟在南，项王故里在老城区。

高铁停靠宿迁站，离市区有一段距离，打车最省事；公交便宜但收班早，夜间班次很少。

湖边与山上风大，春季与秋季最舒服，夏季日晒强，冬季湿冷。

环湖骑行是最划算的玩法，只是沿途补给点不多，出发前要把水带够。

Suqian's sights scatter across three directions: Luoma Lake and Three Terrace Mountain to the north, the distilleries at Yanghe and Shuanggou to the south, Xiang Yu's old home in the old town.

High-speed trains stop at Suqian station, some distance from the centre, and a taxi is the least trouble; buses are cheap but stop early, with few night services.

It is windy by the lake and on the hills; spring and autumn are most comfortable, summer sun is strong and winter is damp and cold.

Cycling around the lake is the best value of all, except that supply points along the route are sparse — carry water before you set off.

Suqian is not a city that wins you over at once: the streets are wide and empty, the new districts tidy to the point of dullness, and the nights are not lively.

Yet the cards it holds are not bad — the home of a man who lost, a lake still being worked, two sets of fermentation pits decades old, a canal still carrying freight.

These things have nothing to do with one another, and yet they point to the same fact: the strata here are thick, and simply not good at saying so.

It does not explain itself. Whether you read it or not is, in the end, your own business.
