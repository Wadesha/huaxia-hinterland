荆州

Jingzhou

一片楚辞从这里起调，一座古城在长江边守了两千年——凤的翅膀，还糊在城砖里。

Chu elegies tuned from here; an old town kept two thousand years by the Yangtse — the phoenix's wings still glue the brick.

01

先别奔荆州博物馆，去古城墙坐坐Arrive on the old wall

荆州不声不响。你一下高铁，城平得没脾气，可往里走，明代砖城猛地亮出来——一座保存完整的城，枕着长江的支流，像平原上的一枚方印。

我第一次懂荆州，是在古城墙的傍晚。护城河的水慢吞吞转，城楼影子在河里晃，远处有人在唱荆楚调。那一刻你明白，这座城把"楚"字写进了砖。

当地贴士：以荆州古城为基地，博物馆、关帝庙、长江都在城内或近旁；住城内，骑车绕城最舒服。
Local tip:Base in the old town; museum, Guandi temple, Yangtse all in or near town — stay inside, bike the ring.

Jingzhou keeps quiet. Step off the train and the city is flat without temper, yet walk in and the Ming brick wall lights sudden — a complete city, pillow on the Yangtse's branch, a square seal on the plain.

I got Jingzhou at dusk on the old wall. The moat turns slow, the gate tower's shadow sways on the water, someone sang the Jing–Chu tune. That moment you see this city wrote 'Chu' into its brick.

当地贴士：以荆州古城为基地，博物馆、关帝庙、长江都在城内或近旁；住城内，骑车绕城最舒服。
Local tip:Base in the old town; museum, Guandi temple, Yangtse all in or near town — stay inside, bike the ring.
02

荆州到底是什么What Jingzhou is

荆州是楚都故地，春秋战国时楚国的中心，"朝秦暮楚"的楚就在江汉之间。它也是三国名城，关羽丢荆州的故事让这座城成了"大意"的注脚。

长江在城边拐弯，把城和岸硬硬切开。荆州的好，在于它又浪漫又刚硬——一片楚辞能起调，一座城能守两千年。

Jingzhou is the Chu capital land, the Chu heart in the Spring-and-Autumn and Warring States, the 'Chu between Qin and Qin at dawn and dusk' along the Jiang–Han. It's also a Three Kingdoms name; Guan Yu losing Jingzhou made it the footnote of 'carelessness'.

The Yangtse bends by the town, hard-cutting city from bank. Jingzhou's grace is romantic and hard — a Chu elegy tuned here, a city held two thousand years.

03

怎么走Getting around

荆州站在汉宜线上，从武汉过来高铁两小时出头，从宜昌过来约两小时。市内公交慢，打车便宜；古城不大，骑车最舒服。长江边步道免费。

古城在荆北，腿着就能逛核心；博物馆在城西，公交可达。

实用贴士：买票认准官方「铁路 12306」App；古城骑行免费，租点在城门。
Handy tip:Use the official 12306 app; the old town bikes free, rent at the gate.

Jingzhou sits on the Wuhan–Yichang line, just over two hours from Wuhan, about two from Yichang. City buses slow, cabs cheap; the old town small, bike best. The Yangtse path is free.

The old town is north of Jing; walk its core; the museum west of town is by bus.

实用贴士：买票认准官方「铁路 12306」App；古城骑行免费，租点在城门。
Handy tip:Use the official 12306 app; the old town bikes free, rent at the gate.
04

荆州古城与楚The old town and Chu

荆州古城是保存完整的明代砖城，宾阳楼、钟鼓楼都在线上。城墙可登，沿护城河走，对岸的太湖港把城衬得小。城砖里夯着楚都的土——当年楚郢都就在附近。

关羽祠、关帝庙在城内，香火从三国续到今天。你不用买票，沿墙走一圈就是半部楚汉史。

当地贴士：城墙登临看江景；护城河步道免费，傍晚去最舒服。
Local tip:Climb the wall for the view; the moat path is free, best at dusk.

The Jingzhou old town is a complete Ming brick city; Binyang Tower and the drum tower line it. The wall is climbable along the moat; across, Taihu Port makes the town small. The brick is rammed with Chu-capital soil — the Chu Ying capital was near.

The Guan Yu shrine and Guandi temple in town burn incense from the Three Kingdoms to today. No ticket needed; walk the wall and you've read half the Chu–Han history.

当地贴士：城墙登临看江景；护城河步道免费，傍晚去最舒服。
Local tip:Climb the wall for the view; the moat path is free, best at dusk.
05

博物馆与楚漆器The museum and Chu lacquer

荆州博物馆是地级市里少有的硬核馆，楚墓出土的漆器、丝织品、越王剑，件件让世人瞪眼。虎座鸟架鼓是楚人的浪漫——凤鸟驮着鼓，把死也办得飞扬。

馆里冷气足，站久了想披件衣。你不用懂考古，也能被那种"事死如事生"的劲镇住——楚人连地下都要热闹。

实用贴士：博物馆免费；楚漆器展厅细看，每件都是两千年的热闹。
Handy tip:The museum is free; the Chu lacquer hall rewards slow looking, each two thousand years of liveliness.

The Jingzhou Museum is a rare hard-core prefectural museum; Chu-tomb lacquer, silk, the Yue king's sword — each makes the world stare. The tiger-seat bird-rack drum is Chu romance — a phoenix bird carrying the drum, even death flown high.

The hall is cool; stand long and want a layer. You needn't know archaeology to be hushed by 'treat death as life' — Chu folk wanted the underworld lively too.

实用贴士：博物馆免费；楚漆器展厅细看，每件都是两千年的热闹。
Handy tip:The museum is free; the Chu lacquer hall rewards slow looking, each two thousand years of liveliness.
06

吃什么What to eat

荆州的味道在长江和码头的汤里。鱼糕是招牌，鱼肉捶成泥蒸成糕，嫩得像布丁，配高汤是荆楚人的汤哲学。早堂面是大清早的碱水面，码子厚，赶船人一碗下肚就走。

公安锅盔是烤得焦脆的饼，夹牛肉最香。夜市便宜管饱，街头一口锅能香半条街。

当地贴士：鱼糕认老店，嫩度看火候；早堂面赶饭点最旺，凉了摊收。
Local tip:Fish cake from old names, tenderness by heat; zaotang noodle peaks at meal times, stalls fold when cool.

Jingzhou's flavour is in the Yangtse and dock soup. Fish cake is the signboard — fish pounded to paste, steamed, pudding-soft, with broth the Jing–Chu soup philosophy. Zaotang noodle is dawn alkali noodle, thick topping, one bowl and the boat folk go.

Gong'an guokui is a crisp baked cake, best with beef. The night market cheap and filling; one street pot scents half a lane.

当地贴士：鱼糕认老店，嫩度看火候；早堂面赶饭点最旺，凉了摊收。
Local tip:Fish cake from old names, tenderness by heat; zaotang noodle peaks at meal times, stalls fold when cool.
07

懂点规矩Know the ropes

古城墙、博物馆是庄重之地，进门手机静音，别在展厅里大声。楚漆器是国宝，玻璃柜外看，别敲。三国是荆州的骄傲，可别拿"关羽是不是真的"去戳——这座城把"守"当骨，你客气点，他们能把每场仗说给你听。

江边钓鱼是市民乐子，你在旁看就行，别抢竿。

The old wall and museum are solemn — silence phones, no loud talk in halls. Chu lacquer is national treasure; view through glass, don't tap. The Three Kingdoms is Jingzhou's pride, yet don't poke with 'was Guan Yu real' — the city takes 'hold' as bone, be polite and they'll tell you each battle.

Riverside angling is citizens' joy; watch, don't grab the rod.

08

什么时候去When to go

春天护城河新绿，古城舒服。秋天天高云淡，楚漆器配蓝天最好看。夏天热，江边风大稍好。冬天冷，可雪后古城肃穆，城墙白砖黑瓦像一幅水墨。

若为了楚气质，春秋最对；若为了清静，冬天人最少。

实用贴士：夏天江边带水；冬天砖滑，慢行。
Handy tip:Summer riverside, bring water; winter brick slick, go slow.

Spring greens the moat, the old town ease. Autumn high and clear, Chu lacquer with blue sky best. Summer hot, riverside wind a bit better. Winter cold, yet snow on the old town is ink-painting, white brick black tile.

For the Chu mood, spring–autumn fit; for quiet, winter fewest.

实用贴士：夏天江边带水；冬天砖滑，慢行。
Handy tip:Summer riverside, bring water; winter brick slick, go slow.
09

去哪悠走Where to wander

护城河是免费的天然步道，骑车绕城，宾阳楼、关帝庙都在沿线。城内胡同随便钻，青砖门关着的故事比开着的多。

长江大堤也免费，夕阳把水染成橘色，骑车比走路爽。荆州的好走，在于城把整座楚都摁成了一条线。

实用贴士：护城河骑车免费；长江大堤顺堤走别下水。
Handy tip:The moat bike is free; walk the Yangtse embankment, don't enter water.

The moat is a free path; bike the ring and Binyang Tower, Guandi temple line the way. The inner alleys are yours to wander; more stories behind closed blue-brick doors than open.

The Yangtse embankment is free too; at dusk it dyes the water orange, better by bike. Jingzhou's joy is the town stringing the Chu capital on one line.

实用贴士：护城河骑车免费；长江大堤顺堤走别下水。
Handy tip:The moat bike is free; walk the Yangtse embankment, don't enter water.
10

不花钱也值得Worth it free

荆州最贵的东西，大多不要钱。护城河的风、古城在暮色里的轮廓、江边那阵荆楚调，站着看就是参与。雪后古城配红灯，比收费园里还好看。

江边大爷们唱戏不收钱，你站着听就是捧场。荆州教会我：好地方不靠门票证明自己。

当地贴士：护城河步道免费；博物馆多周一闭馆，去前查开放日。
Local tip:The moat path is free; many museums close Mondays — check first.

Jingzhou's priciest things mostly cost nothing. The moat's wind, the old town's dusk outline, the Jing–Chu tune beyond — stand and you're in it. Snow on the old town with red lamps beats any paid garden.

Old men sing opera free by the river; stand and listen, that's applause. Jingzhou taught me: good places don't prove themselves with a ticket.

当地贴士：护城河步道免费；博物馆多周一闭馆，去前查开放日。
Local tip:The moat path is free; many museums close Mondays — check first.
11

收束Before you leave

荆州不会一下子把你看服。它没有名山的名气，也没有水乡的精巧。它古，它刚，它又楚又守——一片楚辞能从这起调，一座城能守两千年。

你逛过古城的一圈方墙、博物馆的一架楚鼓、长江边的一阵风，临走会明白：这片土地最舍得给的，从来不是名气，而是那种"凤的翅膀还糊在砖里"的浪漫。它不急着留你，却总让你惦记。

Jingzhou won't win you at once. No famous mountain's fame, no water town's finesse. It's ancient, hard, Chu and holding — a Chu elegy tuned here, a city held two thousand years.

You'll see the square wall's ring, the museum's Chu drum, a wind by the Yangtse — and leaving you'll get it: what this land gives most freely was never fame, but the romance of 'the phoenix's wings still glue the brick'. It won't rush to keep you, yet always leaves you missing it.
