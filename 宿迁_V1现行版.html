宿迁

Suqian

一座很新的地级市，底下压着很旧的底子——项羽在这儿出生，酒在这儿酿了两千多年。

A very new prefecture-level city sitting on very old ground — Xiang Yu was born here, and liquor has been made here for two thousand years.

01

先说清楚这是哪First, where this is

宿迁是江苏最年轻的地级市之一，1996 年才从老淮阴分出来。

你在地图上看，它夹在徐州、连云港和淮安之间。

城里到处是新的路和新的楼，招牌上写着"项王故里""中国酒都"。

别被这些新东西骗了，这地方的老底子比你想的深。

你先去老城区走一圈，节奏会慢下来。

Suqian is one of Jiangsu's youngest prefecture-level cities, split off from the old Huaiyin prefecture only in 1996.

On the map it is wedged between Xuzhou, Lianyungang and Huai'an.

The town is full of new roads and new blocks, with signs reading "home of King Xiang" and "China's liquor capital".

Don't be fooled by the newness — what lies underneath is older than you would guess.

Walk the old centre first and the pace drops.

02

项王故里：一个败者的老家Home of a king who lost

项羽出生在下相，也就是今天的宿迁。

项王故里的院子是后来修的，格局规整，柏树很老。

院里有一棵老槐，相传是他亲手栽的，树干粗得要两人合抱。

你站在树下会想，这个人打到最后一刻也不肯过江。

本地人讲他，语气里没有成败，只有熟。

Xiang Yu was born in Xiaxiang, which is Suqian today.

The compound at Xiang Yu's old home is a later rebuild, formally laid out, with very old cypresses.

In the courtyard stands an ancient locust tree said to have been planted by his own hand, its trunk too thick for one pair of arms.

Standing under it, you think of a man who fought to the last and refused to cross the river.

Locals speak of him without any sense of winning or losing, only familiarity.

03

骆马湖：一片大水Luoma Lake: a wide water

骆马湖在城北，湖面很大，风一吹就起浪。

湖边有湿地公园，芦苇长得比人高。

你可以沿湖骑车，路平，人少，鸟多。

渔民还在湖上干活，船很小，网很大。

天好的时候，落日把整片水面压成一条金线。

Luoma Lake lies north of the city, a wide sheet of water that lifts into waves as soon as the wind gets up.

There is a wetland park along the shore where the reeds grow taller than a person.

You can cycle the lake road: flat, almost empty, full of birds.

Fishermen still work the lake in small boats with very large nets.

On a clear day the low sun presses the whole surface down into a single gold line.

04

皂河：一座给龙王盖的行宫Zaohe: a palace built for a river god

皂河镇在城西北，镇上有座很大的庙。

庙是给龙王盖的，后来乾隆南巡住过，所以也叫行宫。

你走进去，第一进是庙，后面几进是宫殿格局，规格高得反常。

梁上的彩画旧了，颜色还在。

这种"庙里套宫殿"的怪样子，全国都不多见。

Zaohe town is northwest of the city, and it has a very large temple.

It was built for the dragon king, and the Qianlong emperor stayed here on his southern tours, which is why it is also called a travelling palace.

Go in and the first hall is a temple, while the halls behind are laid out as a palace — an oddly high specification.

The painted beams have faded but the colour holds.

A palace tucked inside a temple is not something you see often anywhere in the country.

05

酒：这座城的另一半Liquor: the other half of the city

宿迁有两个名酒：洋河和双沟，一个在城南，一个在泗洪。

酒厂很大，厂区里有自己的路和树。

你参观的时候先闻到味，然后才看见窖池。

老窖池的泥是黑的，据说几十年没换过。

本地人喝酒不看牌子，看谁家当年出的货好。

Suqian has two famous liquors: Yanghe and Shuanggou, one south of the city, one in Sihong.

The distilleries are enormous, with their own roads and their own trees inside the gates.

On a visit you smell it first, and only then see the fermentation pits.

The mud in the old pits is black, and they say it has not been changed in decades.

Locals do not drink by label; they drink by which year's batch turned out well.

06

三台山与衲田Three Terrace Mountain and the patchwork fields

三台山在城北，不高，林子密。

山下的衲田是一片拼起来的花田，一块一块，颜色不同。

你沿着田埂走，两边是花，远处是水。

花开的时候人多，不开的时候也照样好看，看的是田的形状。

这里适合慢慢走，不适合赶。

Three Terrace Mountain is north of the city, low and thickly wooded.

Below it the patchwork fields are a mosaic of flower plots, block by block, each a different colour.

You walk the earth ridges with flowers on both sides and water in the distance.

When the blooms come the crowds come too, but it looks just as good without them — it is the shape of the fields you are looking at.

This is a place for walking slowly, not for hurrying.

07

下草湾：几万年前的人Xiacaowan: people from tens of thousands of years ago

泗洪有处下草湾遗址，几十年前出土过古人类的骨头。

化石不多，说法不少，年代以万年计。

现场没什么可看的，就是一片草地和一个说明牌。

你站在那儿，脚下是几万年前有人活动过的土层。

这种地方的价值不在眼睛，在时间。

At Sihong there is the Xiacaowan site, where bones of early humans were dug up some decades ago.

The fossils are few and the theories many, and the date runs in tens of thousands of years.

There is little to see on site: a patch of grass and an explanatory board.

Stand there and the soil under your feet is ground where people were active tens of thousands of years ago.

The value of a place like this is not in what you see, but in how deep the time runs.

08

运河从城边过The canal passes the edge of town

京杭大运河从宿迁穿过去，水是活的，船是动的。

你能看见拖船一长串地走，一艘拖着十几艘。

河边修了步道，入夜有人散步，也有人在钓鱼。

这条河把这座城和更南更北的地方连在一起。

它老得不能再老了，却还在干活。

The Grand Canal runs through Suqian, the water alive and the boats moving.

You see tug trains passing in long strings, one boat pulling a dozen behind it.

A path has been built along the bank; people walk it and people fish from it.

The river ties this city to places much further south and much further north.

It is as old as anything can be, and it is still working.

09

吃：烧鸡、糁汤与面Chicken, soup and noodles

宿迁的吃偏北方，面食多，口味重。

符离集烧鸡的名气在隔壁安徽，可这边也吃，肉烂骨酥。

糁汤是过早的东西，胡椒重，配馍或者油条。

你要是能吃辣，就尝尝小鱼熬辣椒，很下饭。

价钱都不高，一碗一盘，吃得人发困。

实用贴士：洋河酒厂的参观通常要走预约流程，散客先打听当日是否开放；酒都的餐馆入夜生意最好，去晚了要等位。
Handy tip:Visits to the Yanghe distillery generally run on an appointment system, so ask whether it is open to individuals that day. Restaurants here do their best trade at night, and late means queuing.

Suqian food leans north: plenty of wheat, and flavours that do not hold back.

Fuliji roast chicken is famous over the border in Anhui, but it is eaten here too, the meat falling off the bone.

Sa tang is a breakfast thing, heavy with pepper, taken with bread or fried dough.

If you can take chilli, try small fish stewed with it — it goes a long way with rice.

None of it costs much; a bowl and a plate and you are ready for a nap.

实用贴士：洋河酒厂的参观通常要走预约流程，散客先打听当日是否开放；酒都的餐馆入夜生意最好，去晚了要等位。

10

几件要紧事A few things that matter

宿迁的景点分得散，湖在北，酒厂在南，项王故里在老城。

你按方向排，别来回跑。

高铁站是宿迁站，离市区有段路，打车最省事。

城里公交便宜，但收班早，别玩到太晚。

湖边和山上风大，春天和秋天最舒服。

实用贴士：项王故里、皂河龙王庙、三台山分别位于老城、西北与城北，公交可达但耗时；骆马湖环湖路适合骑行，沿途补给点少，出发前带水。
Handy tip:Xiang Yu's old home, the Zaohe dragon-king temple and Three Terrace Mountain sit in the old town, the northwest and the north respectively — reachable by bus but slow. The loop road around Luoma Lake suits cycling, though supply points are few, so carry water.

Suqian's sights point different ways: the lake north, the distilleries south, Xiang Yu's old home in the old town.

Group them by direction; don't keep doubling back.

The high-speed station is Suqian, some way out of town, and a taxi is the least trouble.

Buses in the city are cheap but stop running early, so don't stay out too late.

It is windy by the lake and on the hills; spring and autumn are the comfortable seasons.

实用贴士：项王故里、皂河龙王庙、三台山分别位于老城、西北与城北，公交可达但耗时；骆马湖环湖路适合骑行，沿途补给点少，出发前带水。

11

离开的时候On leaving

宿迁不热闹，也不难看。

它是那种你路过时会想"原来这儿也有东西"的城。

一个败了的霸王，一湖水，几口老窖，一条还在跑船的运河。

这些东西凑在一起，不响，但压得住。

你走的时候大概不会拍照，但会记得味道。

Suqian is not lively, and it is not ugly either.

It is the kind of city where you pass through and think: so there is something here after all.

A king who lost, a lake, a few old fermentation pits, and a canal still carrying boats.

Put together they make no noise, but they hold their ground.

You probably won't take photographs on the way out, but you will remember the smell.
