天水

Tianshui

伏羲画卦的故里，陇山脚下的江南——一片佛窟贴在悬崖上，像大地睁开的眼。

Fuxi's trigram hometown, Jiangnan at the foot of Long Mountain — cliff Buddha caves like the earth's open eyes.

01

先别奔麦积山，去藉河边坐坐Arrive by the Ji River

天水不声不响。你一下高铁，城藏在陇山脚下的河谷里，绿得不像西北。藉河慢流，两边柳成行，像江南被人搬到了黄土沟里。

我第一次懂天水，是在藉河的傍晚。雾从麦积山那边漫过来，远处有人在唱陇调，风把湿润味吹得一层层斜。那一刻你明白，这座城把"佛"和"绿"都供在了门口。

当地贴士：以天水为基地，麦积山、伏羲庙、仙人崖都在 1 小时内；住城内，藉河最舒服。
Local tip:Base in town; Maiji, Fuxi temple, Immortal Cliff within an hour — stay in town, Ji River best.

Tianshui keeps quiet. Step off the train and the city hides in a valley at Long Mountain's foot, green unlike the northwest. The Ji River flows slow, willows in rows, as if Jiangnan moved into a loess gully.

I got Tianshui at dusk by the Ji. Mist spread from Maiji, someone sang the Long tune, wind slanted the damp smell. That moment you see this city enshrined both 'Buddha' and 'green' at its gate.

当地贴士：以天水为基地，麦积山、伏羲庙、仙人崖都在 1 小时内；住城内，藉河最舒服。
Local tip:Base in town; Maiji, Fuxi temple, Immortal Cliff within an hour — stay in town, Ji River best.
02

天水到底是什么What Tianshui is

天水传为伏羲故里，卦台山是画八卦的地方，华夏最早的"数"从这片河谷起。它也是"陇上江南"，藉河、渭河在城边交汇，山抱水绕，绿得不像甘肃。

麦积山的佛贴在悬崖上，一尊尊微笑俯视河谷。天水的好，在于它又祖又佛——一片黄土能养出最早的卦，一座山能供出最软的笑。

Tianshui is told to be Fuxi's home; Guatai Mountain is where the eight trigrams were drawn, Huaxia's earliest 'number' from this valley. It's also 'Jiangnan on Long' — the Ji and Wei rivers meet by town, hills holding water, green unlike Gansu.

Maiji's Buddhas cling to the cliff, smiling down at the valley. Tianshui's grace is ancestor and Buddha — one loess bred the earliest trigram, one mountain offered the softest smile.

03

怎么走Getting around

天水南站在徐兰高铁上，从兰州过来高铁两小时出头，从西安过来约三小时。市内公交慢，打车便宜；麦积山在城东南有公交，伏羲庙在城内。

藉河在城内，腿着或骑车就能到；仙人崖路程远赶早出门。

实用贴士：买票认准官方「铁路 12306」App；去麦积山公交可达，赶早。
Handy tip:Use the official 12306 app; Maiji is by bus, go early.

Tianshui South on the Lanzhou–Lanzhou line is just over two hours from Lanzhou, about three from Xi'an. City buses slow, cabs cheap; Maiji southeast of town has a bus, Fuxi temple in town.

The Ji is in town, walk or bike; Immortal Cliff far, leave earlier.

实用贴士：买票认准官方「铁路 12306」App；去麦积山公交可达，赶早。
Handy tip:Use the official 12306 app; Maiji is by bus, go early.
04

麦积山石窟The Maiji Grottoes

麦积山在城东南，一座孤峰像农家积麦的垛，佛窟贴在崖壁上，栈道悬空，一步一惊。造像从北魏到宋，微笑一千年没变——好像再大的乱，佛都见过。

你不用懂佛教，也能被那种"悬崖也会笑"的劲镇住。窟里恒凉，站久了想披件衣。

当地贴士：麦积山门票含栈道；窟里恒凉带外套，别用闪光灯。
Local tip:Maiji ticket covers the plank path; caves cool, a layer, no flash.

Maiji southeast of town, a lone peak like a farm wheat stack, Buddha caves cling to the cliff, plank paths hanging, a start each step. Images from Northern Wei to Song, smile unchanged a thousand years — as if no turmoil escaped the Buddha.

You needn't know Buddhism to be hushed by 'a cliff can smile'. The caves stay cool; stand long and want a layer.

当地贴士：麦积山门票含栈道；窟里恒凉带外套，别用闪光灯。
Local tip:Maiji ticket covers the plank path; caves cool, a layer, no flash.
05

伏羲庙与卦台山Fuxi Temple and Guatai Mountain

伏羲庙在城内，古柏成林，殿里供着"人文始祖"。卦台山在城外，相传伏羲在此仰观天象、俯察地理，画出八卦。你站在台上，会懂为什么这片土把"数"当呼吸。

庙会时节，方圆几百里的人来"朝祖"，包袱里装着自家蒸的馍。一座庙，把"源"活成了一种姿势——不争，却什么都留住了。

实用贴士：伏羲庙免费或低价；卦台山顺山走别折枝。
Handy tip:Fuxi Temple free or cheap; Guatai follow the hill, don't break branches.

The Fuxi Temple in town, old cypress a forest, enshrines the 'cultural ancestor'. Guatai Mountain outside town is where Fuxi, legend says, watched heaven and earth to draw the eight trigrams. Stand on the terrace and get why this soil breathes 'number'.

At fair time folk from hundreds of li come 'to court the ancestor', steamed buns in packs. One temple lived 'source' as a posture — contending nothing, keeping all.

实用贴士：伏羲庙免费或低价；卦台山顺山走别折枝。
Handy tip:Fuxi Temple free or cheap; Guatai follow the hill, don't break branches.
06

吃什么What to eat

天水的味道在陇上的汤里。呱呱是招牌，荞麦糊凉了切块，浇辣子蒜汁，是一碗的功夫哲学。浆水面是发酵酸汤配细面，夏天最爽。

清真风味浓，手抓羊肉、牛肉饼都实在。夜市便宜管饱，街头一口锅能香半条街。

当地贴士：呱呱认老店，辣度看个人；浆水面夏天最爽，凉了酸。
Local tip:Guagua from old names, heat to taste; jiangshui noodle best in summer, cool turns sour.

Tianshui's flavour is in the Long soup. Guagua is the signboard — buckwheat paste cooled and cubed, with chilli and garlic juice, a bowl's philosophy of skill. Jiangshui noodle is fermented sour soup with thin noodles, best in summer.

Halal flavour is strong; hand-grabbed mutton and beef cake are honest. The night market cheap and filling; one street pot scents half a lane.

当地贴士：呱呱认老店，辣度看个人；浆水面夏天最爽，凉了酸。
Local tip:Guagua from old names, heat to taste; jiangshui noodle best in summer, cool turns sour.
07

懂点规矩Know the ropes

麦积山、伏羲庙是庄重圣地，进门手机静音，别在窟里大声。佛和祖都怕汗，别伸手摸石壁。栈道悬空，别探身拍照。

天水人朴，问路指得清，可别拿"伏羲是不是真的"去戳——这座城把"源"当呼吸，你客气点，他们能把每卦说给你听。

Maiji and Fuxi Temple are solemn — silence phones, no loud talk in caves. Buddha and ancestor dislike sweat; don't touch stone. The plank path hangs; don't lean for shots.

Tianshui people are plain; they point clear, just don't poke with 'was Fuxi real' — the city breathes 'source', be polite and they'll tell you each trigram.

08

什么时候去When to go

春天藉河新绿，麦积山舒服。秋天天高云淡，石窟配蓝天最好看。夏天凉，是西北的空调。冬天冷，可雪后麦积山肃穆，伏羲庙人少清静。

若为了佛与祖，春秋最对；若为了清静，冬天人最少。

实用贴士：夏天窟里带外套；冬天栈道结冰，慢行。
Handy tip:Summer caves, a layer; winter plank ice, go slow.

Spring greens the Ji, Maiji ease. Autumn high and clear, the grottoes with blue sky best. Summer cool, the northwest's AC. Winter cold, yet snow makes Maiji solemn, Fuxi quiet.

For Buddha and ancestor, spring–autumn fit; for quiet, winter fewest.

实用贴士：夏天窟里带外套；冬天栈道结冰，慢行。
Handy tip:Summer caves, a layer; winter plank ice, go slow.
09

去哪悠走Where to wander

藉河是免费的天然步道，骑车沿河，伏羲庙、古城都在沿线。城内胡同随便钻，青砖门关着的故事比开着的多。

仙人崖在城东南，丹霞配石窟，站着看就是半部地质史。天水的好走，在于"佛"和"绿"把整片陇山摁成了一条线。

实用贴士：藉河步道免费；仙人崖顺沟走别下水。
Handy tip:The Ji path is free; Immortal Cliff follow the gully, don't enter water.

The Ji is a free path; bike it and Fuxi Temple, the old town line the way. The inner alleys are yours to wander; more stories behind closed blue-brick doors than open.

Immortal Cliff southeast of town, danxia with grottoes, stand and you've read half a geological history. Tianshui's joy is 'Buddha' and 'green' stringing Long Mountain on one line.

实用贴士：藉河步道免费；仙人崖顺沟走别下水。
Handy tip:The Ji path is free; Immortal Cliff follow the gully, don't enter water.
10

不花钱也值得Worth it free

天水最贵的东西，大多不要钱。藉河的风、麦积山在暮色里的轮廓、伏羲庙外那阵陇调，站着看就是参与。雪后麦积山配红灯，比收费园里还好看。

城边大爷们念"人祖爷"不收钱，你站着听就是捧场。天水教会我：好地方不靠门票证明自己。

当地贴士：藉河步道免费；博物馆多周一闭馆，去前查开放日。
Local tip:The Ji path is free; many museums close Mondays — check first.

Tianshui's priciest things mostly cost nothing. The Ji's wind, Maiji's dusk outline, the Long tune beyond Fuxi — stand and you're in it. Snow on Maiji with red lamps beats any paid garden.

Old men recite 'ancestor grandfather' free by the town; stand and listen, that's applause. Tianshui taught me: good places don't prove themselves with a ticket.

当地贴士：藉河步道免费；博物馆多周一闭馆，去前查开放日。
Local tip:The Ji path is free; many museums close Mondays — check first.
11

收束Before you leave

天水不会一下子把你看服。它没有名山的名气，也没有水乡的精巧。它绿，它佛，它又祖又软——一片黄土能养出最早的卦，一座山能供出最软的笑。

你逛过麦积山的一窟笑佛、卦台山的一画八卦、藉河边的一阵风，临走会明白：这片土地最舍得给的，从来不是名气，而是那种"佛在悬崖上，绿在黄土里"的柔。它不急着留你，却总让你惦记。

Tianshui won't win you at once. No famous mountain's fame, no water town's finesse. It's green, Buddhist, ancestor and soft — one loess bred the earliest trigram, one mountain offered the softest smile.

You'll see Maiji's smiling Buddha, Guatai's drawn trigram, a wind by the Ji — and leaving you'll get it: what this land gives most freely was never fame, but the softness of 'Buddha on the cliff, green in the loess'. It won't rush to keep you, yet always leaves you missing it.
