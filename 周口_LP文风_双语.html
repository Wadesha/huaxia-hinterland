周口

Zhoukou

道家从这儿起笔，伏羲在淮阳画下第一卦——一片平原，供着两个"祖"。

Taoism penned its first line here; Fuxi drew the first trigram at Huaiyang — one plain enshrines two 'ancestors'.

01

先别奔太昊陵，去淮阳湖边坐坐Arrive by Huaiyang lake

周口不声不响。你一下高铁，城平得没脾气，可往东到淮阳，龙湖猛地铺开——一座太昊陵泡在水里，像北方怀里的一枚江南。

我第一次懂周口，是在淮阳龙湖的傍晚。芦苇荡里鸟起起落落，远处太昊陵的香火味飘过来，有人用方言念"人祖爷"。那一刻你明白，这座城把"祖"供成了日常的呼吸。

当地贴士：以淮阳(龙湖·太昊陵)为重心，鹿邑(老子故里)在 1 小时车程；住淮阳，湖边最舒服。
Local tip:Center on Huaiyang (Dragon Lake, Fuxi Mausoleum); Lùyì (Laozi's home) is an hour — stay in Huaiyang, lakeside best.

Zhoukou keeps quiet. Step off the train and the city is flat without temper, yet east at Huaiyang the Dragon Lake spreads sudden — the Fuxi Mausoleum soaked in water, Jiangnan in the north's arms.

I got Zhoukou at dusk on Huaiyang's Dragon Lake. Birds rose and fell in the reeds, the Fuxi Mausoleum's incense drifted over, someone recited 'ancestor grandfather' in dialect. That moment you see this city breathes 'ancestor' as daily air.

当地贴士：以淮阳(龙湖·太昊陵)为重心，鹿邑(老子故里)在 1 小时车程；住淮阳，湖边最舒服。
Local tip:Center on Huaiyang (Dragon Lake, Fuxi Mausoleum); Lùyì (Laozi's home) is an hour — stay in Huaiyang, lakeside best.
02

周口到底是什么What Zhoukou is

周口是老子故里——鹿邑出李耳，道家的第一笔从这片平原起。它还是伏羲的淮阳，太昊陵是"人祖"的坟，华夏最早的卦象从龙湖边画下。一片土，供着道祖和人祖两个"祖"。

沙河、颍河、贾鲁河在城边汇成"周口"，三水养出的市井，又土又活。

Zhoukou is Laozi's home — Lùyì bore Li Er, Taoism's first stroke from this plain. It's also Fuxi's Huaiyang; the Fuxi Mausoleum is the 'human ancestor's' tomb, China's earliest trigrams drawn by the Dragon Lake. One soil enshrines Taoist and human ancestors.

The Sha, Ying and Jialu rivers meet as 'Zhoukou'; three waters breed a market both plain and alive.

03

怎么走Getting around

周口东站在郑阜高铁上，从郑州过来高铁一小时出头，从阜阳过来约两小时。市内公交慢，打车便宜；淮阳、鹿邑都通城际公交，景点散。

龙湖在淮阳城内，腿着就能逛；鹿邑老子故里得坐县际车。

实用贴士：买票认准官方「铁路 12306」App；淮阳骑车免费，租点在湖边。
Handy tip:Use the official 12306 app; Huaiyang bikes free, rent by the lake.

Zhoukou East on the Zhengzhou–Fuyang line is just over an hour from Zhengzhou, about two from Fuyang. City buses slow, cabs cheap; Huaiyang and Lùyì link by intercity bus, sights spread.

The Dragon Lake is inside Huaiyang, walkable; Laozi's home in Lùyì needs a county bus.

实用贴士：买票认准官方「铁路 12306」App；淮阳骑车免费，租点在湖边。
Handy tip:Use the official 12306 app; Huaiyang bikes free, rent by the lake.
04

淮阳：伏羲与龙湖Huaiyang: Fuxi and the Dragon Lake

淮阳龙湖是平原上罕见的大湖，太昊陵在湖心岛上，香火从远古续到今天。庙会时节，方圆几百里的人来"朝祖"，包袱里装着自家蒸的馍。

湖边芦苇成荡，鸟比人多。你不用买票，沿湖走一圈就是半部华夏起源史。伏羲画卦的台子还在，石头被摸得发亮。

当地贴士：太昊陵庙会人极多，想清静赶非会期；龙湖步道免费。
Local tip:The Fuxi fair crowds hugely; for quiet come off-season; the lake path is free.

Huaiyang's Dragon Lake is a rare plain lake; the Fuxi Mausoleum on the lake-island has burned incense from antiquity to today. At fair time, folk from hundreds of li come 'to court the ancestor', steamed buns in their packs.

Reeds make a marsh, birds outnumber people. No ticket needed; walk the lake and you've read half the Huaxia origin. The platform where Fuxi drew trigrams remains, stone rubbed shiny.

当地贴士：太昊陵庙会人极多，想清静赶非会期；龙湖步道免费。
Local tip:The Fuxi fair crowds hugely; for quiet come off-season; the lake path is free.
05

鹿邑：老子的家Lùyì: Laozi's home

鹿邑是李耳的出生地，太清宫供着"道祖"。古柏成林，唐槐宋碑，空气里都是"道法自然"的慢。老子写《道德经》时，大概就坐在这片平原上发呆。

明道宫在城内，老君台是当年传孔子问礼的地方。你站着看古柏，会懂为什么道家把"无为"活成了一种姿势——不争，却什么都留住了。

实用贴士：太清宫免费或低价；古柏林免费逛，别折枝。
Handy tip:Taiqing Palace is free or cheap; the cypress grove free, don't break branches.

Lùyì is Li Er's birthplace; the Taiqing Palace enshrines the 'Taoist ancestor'. Old cypress a forest, Tang locust and Song steles, air slow with 'the way follows nature'. When Laozi wrote the Daodejing he likely sat on this plain, daydreaming.

The Mingdao Palace in town, the Laojun Terrace where Confucius reportedly asked rites. Stand by the old cypress and get why Taoism lived 'non-action' as a posture — contending nothing, yet keeping all.

实用贴士：太清宫免费或低价；古柏林免费逛，别折枝。
Handy tip:Taiqing Palace is free or cheap; the cypress grove free, don't break branches.
06

吃什么What to eat

周口的味道在沙颍河的汤里。逍遥镇胡辣汤是招牌，牛骨熬的稠汤，胡椒八角冲得通透，配水煎包是豫东的汤哲学。邓城猪蹄卤得油亮，凉吃下酒最妙。

沈丘顾家馍是本地面点，蒸成小人小兽，松软带甜。街头一口锅能香半条街，便宜管饱。

当地贴士：胡辣汤趁热喝，辣度看个人；水煎包现煎最香，底脆。
Local tip:Hot pepper soup hot, heat to taste; pan-fried buns best fresh, crisp bottom.

Zhoukou's flavour is in the Sha–Ying river soup. Xiaoyao town's hot pepper soup is the signboard — beef-bone thick broth, pepper and star anise cleanly hot, with pan-fried buns the soup philosophy of east Henan. Dengcheng pig trotter is glossy braised, best cold with liquor.

Shenqiu Gu family mantou is local dough, steamed to little people and beasts, soft and sweet. One street pot scents half a lane, cheap and filling.

当地贴士：胡辣汤趁热喝，辣度看个人；水煎包现煎最香，底脆。
Local tip:Hot pepper soup hot, heat to taste; pan-fried buns best fresh, crisp bottom.
07

懂点规矩Know the ropes

太昊陵、太清宫是庄重圣地，进门手机静音，别在殿里大声。伏羲、老子都讲"敬"，香小心点，别踩门槛。庙会人多，背包前抱防挤。

周口人朴，问路指得清，可别拿"迷信不迷信"去戳——这座城把"祖"当呼吸，你客气点，他们能把每炷香的来路说给你听。

The Fuxi Mausoleum and Taiqing Palace are solemn shrines — silence phones, no loud talk in halls. Both Fuxi and Laozi teach 'reverence'; mind the incense, don't step thresholds. Fairs crowd; hold your pack front.

Zhoukou people are plain; they point clear, just don't poke with 'superstition or not' — the city breathes 'ancestor', be polite and they'll tell you each stick's road.

08

什么时候去When to go

春天龙湖新绿，淮阳舒服。二月太昊陵庙会最盛，人山人海但气氛热。秋天天高云淡，鹿邑古柏配蓝天最好看。冬天冷，可雪后龙湖肃穆，太清宫人少清静。

若为了"祖"的气，庙会最浓；若为了清静，冬春最好。

实用贴士：庙会赶早别赶晚；冬天湖风大，围巾管用。
Handy tip:Fair: earlier not later; winter lake wind, a scarf helps.

Spring greens the Dragon Lake, Huaiyang ease. The second-month Fuxi fair peaks, seas of people yet warm. Autumn high and clear, Lùyì's old cypress with blue sky best. Winter cold, yet snow makes the lake solemn, Taiqing quiet.

For the 'ancestor' mood the fair is thickest; for quiet, winter–spring best.

实用贴士：庙会赶早别赶晚；冬天湖风大，围巾管用。
Handy tip:Fair: earlier not later; winter lake wind, a scarf helps.
09

去哪悠走Where to wander

龙湖是免费的天然步道，骑车绕湖，太昊陵、画卦台都在沿线。湖心岛的小巷随便钻，青砖门关着的故事比开着的多。

鹿邑古柏林免费，站着听风就是半部道史。周口的好走，在于"祖"把整座城摁成了一条线。

实用贴士：龙湖骑车免费；鹿邑古柏林顺林走别折枝。
Handy tip:The lake bike is free; Lùyì grove walk, don't break branches.

The Dragon Lake is a free path; bike it and the Fuxi Mausoleum, the trigram platform line the way. The island's lanes are yours to wander; more stories behind closed blue-brick doors than open.

Lùyì's cypress grove is free; stand and hear wind — half a Taoist history. Zhoukou's joy is 'ancestor' stringing the city on one line.

实用贴士：龙湖骑车免费；鹿邑古柏林顺林走别折枝。
Handy tip:The lake bike is free; Lùyì grove walk, don't break branches.
10

不花钱也值得Worth it free

周口最贵的东西，大多不要钱。龙湖的风、太昊陵在暮色里的香火、鹿邑古柏林的一阵风，站着看就是参与。雪后龙湖配红灯，比收费园里还好看。

湖边大爷们念"人祖爷"不收钱，你站着听就是捧场。周口教会我：好地方不靠门票证明自己。

当地贴士：龙湖步道免费；太清宫非会期人少清静。
Local tip:The lake path is free; Taiqing quiet off-season.

Zhoukou's priciest things mostly cost nothing. The Dragon Lake's wind, the Fuxi Mausoleum's dusk incense, Lùyì's cypress wind — stand and you're in it. Snow on the lake with red lamps beats any paid garden.

Old men recite 'ancestor grandfather' free by the lake; stand and listen, that's applause. Zhoukou taught me: good places don't prove themselves with a ticket.

当地贴士：龙湖步道免费；太清宫非会期人少清静。
Local tip:The lake path is free; Taiqing quiet off-season.
11

收束Before you leave

周口不会一下子把你看服。它没有名山的名气，也没有水乡的精巧。它平，它朴，它又道又祖——一部《道德经》能从这起笔，一卦能从这画下。

你逛过淮阳的一湖香火、鹿邑的一林古柏、沙颍河边的一阵风，临走会明白：这片土地最舍得给的，从来不是名气，而是那种"祖供在呼吸里"的稳。它不急着留你，却总让你惦记。

Zhoukou won't win you at once. No famous mountain's fame, no water town's finesse. It's flat, plain, Taoist and ancestral — a Daodejing penned here, a trigram drawn here.

You'll see Huaiyang's lake of incense, Lùyì's cypress grove, a wind by the Sha–Ying — and leaving you'll get it: what this land gives most freely was never fame, but the steadiness of 'ancestor breathed daily'. It won't rush to keep you, yet always leaves you missing it.
