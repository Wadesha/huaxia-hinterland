连云港

Lianyungang

铁路走到尽头就是海——山在城里，港在城外，螃蟹在锅里。

The railway runs out at the sea — hills inside the city, a harbour beyond it, crab in the pot.

01

一条铁路的尽头Where the railway ends

陇海铁路从兰州一路铺到这里，再往前就是海了。

你到连云港，多半是奔着海来的。

可这座城的第一眼不是海，是山——花果山就从市区里长出来。

先别急着上岛，你在老城里转一圈，会听见两种口音，一种带海腥，一种带鲁南的硬。

这座城贴着江苏的最东北角，隔壁就是山东。

The Longhai railway runs all the way from Lanzhou and stops here, because beyond lies the sea.

You come to Lianyungang mostly for the water.

But the first thing you see is not the sea, it is a hill — Huaguo Mountain rises straight out of the town.

Don't rush to the island yet; walk the old streets and you will hear two accents, one salted by the sea, one hard-edged from southern Shandong.

The city sits on Jiangsu's far north-eastern corner, with Shandong next door.

02

花果山：一座山借了个故事A hill that borrowed a story

花果山不高，玉女峰是江苏的最高峰。

你爬上去要一两个小时，路上全是《西游记」的痕迹。

水帘洞是一挂小瀑布，洞里有石桌石凳，说是孙悟空的家。

猴子是真的有。它们坐在台阶上等人喂，也抢人手里的袋子。

故事是借的，山是真的，这一点本地人分得很清楚。

Huaguo Mountain is not high, and its Jade Maiden Peak is the highest point in Jiangsu.

The climb takes an hour or two, and the whole way up is marked with traces of Journey to the West.

Water Curtain Cave is a small fall with stone tables and stools inside, said to be the Monkey King's home.

The monkeys are real. They sit on the steps waiting to be fed, and they snatch bags out of hands.

The story was borrowed, the mountain is real, and locals keep the two firmly apart.

03

连岛：一条堤把海分成了两半A causeway that splits the sea

去连岛要先过大堤。堤很长，车开在上面，两边都是水。

岛上有浴场，沙子粗一些，人也少一些。

你站在礁石上看港口，吊臂一排排立着，货轮一艘艘进出。

这片海不蓝得发亮，它更实在——有船，有腥味，有干活的人。

风大的时候，浪把堤上的护栏打得直响。

Getting to Lian Island means crossing a causeway — a long one, with water on both sides as you drive.

There is a bathing beach on the island; the sand is coarse and the crowd is thin.

Stand on the rocks and look at the port: rows of cranes, container ships moving in and out.

This sea is not a brilliant blue; it is plainer than that, with ships, a fishy smell and people at work.

When the wind gets up, the waves hammer the railings along the causeway.

04

最早的佛像刻在山坡上The earliest Buddhas, cut on a slope

孔望山在城西南，山不高，坡上有一片摩崖造像。

石头刻的是东汉晚期的东西，人物、佛像、还有世俗场面，挤在一面崖壁上。

你凑近看，很多已经模糊了，只剩轮廓。

讲解牌上写着它是中国最早的佛教摩崖造像之一，这个说法学界还在争。

争归争，站在一千八百多年前的凿痕面前，你会安静下来。

Kongwang Mountain is southwest of town, low, with a set of cliff carvings on its slope.

The stone was cut in the late Eastern Han: figures, Buddhas and everyday scenes crowded onto one rock face.

Up close much of it has worn away, and only outlines remain.

The notice board says these are among the earliest Buddhist cliff carvings in China, a claim still argued over by scholars.

Argue as they like — standing in front of chisel marks more than eighteen hundred years old, you go quiet.

05

将军崖：石头上的脸Faces on the rock at Jiangjun Cliff

海州的锦屏山里有一块岩面，叫将军崖。

上面刻着人面、兽面、禾苗和星星，是新石器时代留下来的。

你很难说清它们是什么意思。有人说是祭祀，有人说是观天。

这些刻痕距今几千年，比城里所有的房子都老得多。

崖前风大，山草长得密，很少有人在这儿久留。

In the Jinping hills near Haizhou there is a rock face called Jiangjun Cliff.

Human faces, animal faces, grain and stars are cut into it, left by people of the Neolithic.

Nobody can say for certain what they mean — some call it ritual, some call it sky-watching.

The marks are thousands of years old, far older than anything built in the city.

The wind is strong in front of the cliff, the grass grows thick, and few people linger.

06

港：一座为船而生的城A port first, a city second

连云港的名字就是从港来的，1933 年开港建埠。

你坐公交到港区边上，能看见集装箱堆成彩色的墙。

这里是中国沿海的中转站之一，货物从这里上岸，再往西走。

港区的路很宽，卡车一辆接一辆，尘土也大。

这座城的一半脾气，是被这些钢铁和货车养出来的。

The city is named after its port, which opened in 1933.

Take a bus to the edge of the port area and you see containers stacked into a coloured wall.

This is one of the transfer points on the Chinese coast: goods land here and travel west.

The roads in the port district are wide, the lorries come one after another, and the dust is thick.

Half the city's temperament has been raised on this steel and these trucks.

07

水晶城：东海底下的石头Crystal from under Donghai

东海县在市区西边，以水晶出名。

城里有水晶市场，柜台里摆着原石、手串、雕件，光打上去很晃眼。

你不懂行也能逛，看看颜色，摸摸分量。

真要买，最好找懂的人带着，价格差得很远。

水晶是这地方地底下长出来的东西，也算一种土产。

Donghai county lies west of the city and is known for its crystal.

There is a crystal market in town: rough stone, bracelets, carvings, all of it throwing light around the counters.

You can browse without knowing anything — look at the colour, feel the weight.

If you really want to buy, bring someone who knows; the prices vary wildly.

Crystal grows out of the ground here, which makes it a local crop of sorts.

08

吃：一锅海鲜不加花样Seafood without the flourishes

连云港的海鲜不讲究摆盘，蒸一蒸，煮一煮，端上来就是一大盘。

梭子蟹最当季的时候肉是满的，掰开能看到膏。

沙光鱼是本地的鱼，肉嫩，汤白。

你也可以在街边吃一碗凉粉，配蒜汁和辣椒，几块钱一份。

价钱比大城市便宜，分量也实在，生意都在码头和菜场旁边。

实用贴士：海鲜在墟沟、海棠路一带的店最集中，先问价再点；禁渔期（通常 5 月至 8 月）海鲜品种少、价钱高，介意的话避开。
Handy tip:Seafood places cluster around Xugou and Haitang Road — ask the price before you order. During the fishing ban (generally May to August) choice is thin and prices high, so avoid it if that matters to you.

Lianyungang seafood does not bother with plating: steamed or boiled, then a big plate set in front of you.

When swimming crabs are in season the meat is full, and the roe shows when you crack them open.

Shaguang fish is a local fish, tender-fleshed, and makes a white soup.

You can also eat a bowl of liangfen at a street stall, with garlic water and chilli, for a few yuan.

Prices are lower than in the big cities and portions are honest; the trade sits beside the docks and the markets.

实用贴士：海鲜在墟沟、海棠路一带的店最集中，先问价再点；禁渔期（通常 5 月至 8 月）海鲜品种少、价钱高，介意的话避开。
Handy tip:Seafood places cluster around Xugou and Haitang Road — ask the price before you order. During the fishing ban (generally May to August) choice is thin and prices high, so avoid it if that matters to you.
09

海州老城The old town at Haizhou

海州是这座城的老根，比港区的历史长得多。

老街在修，一边是青砖老屋，一边是新刷的墙。

你拐进小巷，能看见门口晒着的鱼干和咸菜。

城隍庙那一片香火还在，逢日子就有人来。

这里节奏慢，和大港口的忙是两回事。

Haizhou is the old root of this city, far older than the port district.

The old street is being restored: grey-brick houses on one side, freshly painted walls on the other.

Turn into a lane and you see dried fish and pickles set out to air by the door.

The City God temple still has its incense, and people come on the right days.

The pace here is slow, a different thing entirely from the busy port.

10

几件要紧事A few things that matter

连云港的火车站有两个，连云港站离市区近，连云港东站靠港和连岛。

你买票前先看清楚，两地之间还有段距离。

去花果山和连岛都有公交，票价便宜，但要留出等车的时间。

夏天海边的太阳很毒，帽子和水要带上。

海鲜虽好，肠胃弱的别一次吃太多，配点主食稳妥。

实用贴士：连云港站与连云港东站相距约 20 公里，公交与打车均可；花果山有景区交通车，爬玉女峰也可全程步行，来回约 4 小时。
Handy tip:Lianyungang station and Lianyungang East are roughly 20 km apart, reachable by bus or taxi. Huaguo Mountain has a shuttle bus, though the Jade Maiden Peak can be walked both ways — about four hours in total.

There are two railway stations: Lianyungang station near the centre, and Lianyungang East, closer to the port and Lian Island.

Check which one before you buy a ticket; there is real distance between them.

Buses run to both Huaguo Mountain and Lian Island, cheap but not frequent, so allow waiting time.

The sun on the coast is fierce in summer — bring a hat and water.

Good as the seafood is, go easy on it if your stomach is delicate, and order some staples alongside.

11

离开的时候On leaving

连云港不是那种一眼就让人喜欢上的城。

它有点粗，有点乱，风里总是咸的。

可你坐上车以后，会想起那挂小瀑布、那面模糊的石刻，还有一盘没吃完的蟹。

铁路在这儿到头，海在这儿开头。

不来也行，来过就忘不掉那股腥味。

Lianyungang is not a city people fall for at first sight.

It is a little rough, a little untidy, and the wind always tastes of salt.

But once you are on the train you remember the small waterfall, the worn carvings, the plate of crab you didn't finish.

The railway ends here, and the sea begins here.

You can skip it — but that salt smell stays with you once you've been.
