01

先别奔归德府，去城墙上绕圈Arrive on the wall

商丘不抢戏。你一下高铁，城新得没脾气，可往南到睢阳，归德府古城猛地亮出来——一座保存近乎固执的圆形城，外圆内方，像一枚扣在地上的铜钱。

我第一次懂商丘，是在古城墙的傍晚。租辆自行车沿城圈慢慢骑，垛口把夕阳切成齿，远处有人在唱豫东调。那一刻你明白，这座城把"圆"守成了信仰。

当地贴士：以归德府古城为基地，芒砀山、黄河故道都在 1 小时车程；住城内，骑车绕城最舒服。
Local tip:Base in the Gui德府 old town; Mangdang, the old river are within an hour — stay inside, bike the ring.

Shangqiu doesn't show off. Step off the train and the city is new without temper, yet south at Suiyang the Gui德府 old town lights sudden — a round city kept with near stubbornness, outer round inner square, a coin buckled to the ground.

I got Shangqiu at dusk on the wall. Rent a bike, ride the ring slow, crenellations cut the sunset to teeth, someone sang the east-Henan tune. That moment you see this city kept 'round' as faith.

当地贴士：以归德府古城为基地，芒砀山、黄河故道都在 1 小时车程；住城内，骑车绕城最舒服。
Local tip:Base in the Gui德府 old town; Mangdang, the old river are within an hour — stay inside, bike the ring.
02

商丘到底是什么What Shangqiu is

商丘是"三商之源"——商人、商业、商都，三个"商"都从这片土地出发。契（商的始祖）被封于此，后来商族从这里走向王朝。它还是豫东门户，黄河故道从城边斜斜划过。

归德府古城是座圆形城，全国少有。圆的固执，是商丘最本真的脾气——不方不棱，把三千年摁成了一个圈。

Shangqiu is the 'source of three shang' — merchant, commerce, capital, all set out from this soil. Xie (the Shang ancestor) was enfeoffed here; the Shang clan walked from here to dynasty. It's also east-Henan's gate, the Yellow River's old course cutting past.

The Gui德府 old town is round, rare in China. That round stubbornness is Shangqiu's truest temper — no square, no edge, three thousand years pressed to one ring.

03

怎么走Getting around

商丘站在陇海与京九交口，从郑州过来高铁一小时出头，从徐州过来约两小时。市内公交慢，打车便宜；古城不大，骑车最舒服。芒砀山在永城，得坐县际车或包车。

黄河故道在城北，路程远，赶早出门比赶晚从容。

实用贴士：买票认准官方「铁路 12306」App；古城骑车免费，租点在城门。
Handy tip:Use the official 12306 app; the old town bikes free, rent at the gate.

Shangqiu sits where Longhai meets Beijing–Kowloon, just over an hour from Zhengzhou, about two from Xuzhou. City buses slow, cabs cheap; the old town small, bike best. Mangdang in Yongcheng needs a county bus or hired car.

The old river north of town is far; leave earlier, not later.

实用贴士：买票认准官方「铁路 12306」App；古城骑车免费，租点在城门。
Handy tip:Use the official 12306 app; the old town bikes free, rent at the gate.
04

归德府：一座圆的城Gui德府: a round town

归德府古城是全国少有的圆形府城，外圆内方，砖城墙基本完整。四门不对开，街道斜出，像故意不让你走直路——古人信"直路招煞"，圆城能聚气。

城里老宅多是明清院落，木门半掩，大爷在门槛上晒暖。你不用买票，沿城墙走一圈就是半部豫东史。

当地贴士：城墙步行或骑车免费；城内老宅多私产，别擅入。
Local tip:The wall walk or bike is free; inner old homes are private, don't enter uninvited.

The Gui德府 old town is China's rare round prefectural city, outer round inner square, brick wall largely intact. The four gates don't align, streets slant — ancients feared 'straight roads invite evil', a round town gathers qi.

Old homes are Ming–Qing courtyards, wood doors ajar, old men sunning on thresholds. No ticket needed; walk the wall and you've read half east-Henan history.

当地贴士：城墙步行或骑车免费；城内老宅多私产，别擅入。
Local tip:The wall walk or bike is free; inner old homes are private, don't enter uninvited.
05

芒砀山与汉墓Mangdang and the Han tombs

芒砀山在永城，是刘邦斩蛇起义的地方，也是汉梁王墓群所在。墓道深得手机信号都断，石刻壁画在黑暗里讲了两千年。

山不高，却满是好汉和王的戏。梁孝王墓的"黄肠题凑"是汉代最高葬制，你在地下走一圈，会懂为什么古人把"死"也修得这样郑重。

实用贴士：芒砀山门票含墓道；地下信号弱，结伴走别落单。
Handy tip:Mangdang ticket covers the tombs; underground signal weak — go with company, don't straggle.

Mangdang in Yongcheng is where Liu Bang rose by slaying the serpent, and home of the Han Liang royal tombs. The passage runs so deep your signal dies; stone murals tell two thousand years in the dark.

The hill is low yet full of outlaw and king drama. The Liang tomb's 'yellow intestine' timber casing is the Han's highest burial rite; walk underground and you'll see why they built 'death' so solemn.

实用贴士：芒砀山门票含墓道；地下信号弱，结伴走别落单。
Handy tip:Mangdang ticket covers the tombs; underground signal weak — go with company, don't straggle.
06

吃什么What to eat

商丘的味道在豫东的汤里。啥汤是招牌，麦仁鸡骨熬的稠汤，胡椒冲蛋花，辣得人清醒，是豫东人的汤哲学。水激馍是本地甜点——馒头切丁炸了浸糖浆，外脆里软。

垛子羊肉、sha 汤都实在，配烧饼。夜市便宜管饱，街头一口锅能香半条街。

当地贴士：啥汤趁热喝，辣度看个人；水激馍现做最香，凉了皮硬。
Local tip:Sha soup hot, heat to taste; water-fried mantou best fresh, hard when cool.

Shangqiu's flavour is in east-Henan soup. Sha soup is the signboard — wheat-kernel chicken-bone thick broth, pepper and egg, hot enough to wake, the soup philosophy of east Henan. Water-fried mantou is local sweet — diced bun fried then syrup-soaked, crisp out soft in.

Stacked mutton and sha soup are honest with a biscuit. The night market is cheap and filling; one street pot scents half a lane.

当地贴士：啥汤趁热喝，辣度看个人；水激馍现做最香，凉了皮硬。
Local tip:Sha soup hot, heat to taste; water-fried mantou best fresh, hard when cool.
07

懂点规矩Know the ropes

归德府是庄重古城，城墙上别骑车带人晃，别在垛口大声。芒砀山汉墓是地下文物，手机静音，别用闪光灯，别伸手摸石刻。

商丘人守旧，问路指得清，可别拿"圆城是不是迷信"去戳——这座城把"圆"当根，你客气点，他们能把每道门说给你听。

Gui德府 is a solemn old town; on the wall don't bike with a passenger swaying, no loud talk at the crenels. Mangdang's tombs are underground relics — silence phones, no flash, don't touch the carving.

Shangqiu people are traditional; they point clear, just don't poke with 'is the round town superstition' — the city takes 'round' as root, be polite and they'll tell you each gate.

08

什么时候去When to go

春天城墙新绿，古城舒服。秋天天高云淡，芒砀山配蓝天最好看。夏天热，城里有树荫。冬天冷，可雪后圆城肃穆，城墙白砖黑瓦像一幅水墨。

若为了圆城气质，春秋最对；若为了汉墓，四季都成，地下恒温。

实用贴士：夏天城墙带伞；冬天砖滑，慢行。
Handy tip:Summer wall, bring a brolly; winter brick slick, go slow.

Spring greens the wall, the old town ease. Autumn high and clear, Mangdang with blue sky best. Summer hot, town has shade. Winter cold, yet snow on the round town is ink-painting, white brick black tile.

For the round-town mood, spring–autumn fit; for the tombs, any season works, underground stays even.

实用贴士：夏天城墙带伞；冬天砖滑，慢行。
Handy tip:Summer wall, bring a brolly; winter brick slick, go slow.
09

去哪悠走Where to wander

归德府城墙是免费的天然步道，骑车绕城一圈，四门、垛口、护城河都在沿线。城内胡同随便钻，青砖门关着的故事比开着的多。

黄河故道大堤也免费，夕阳把水染成橘色，骑车比走路爽。商丘的好走，在于圆城把整座城摁成了一条线。

实用贴士：城墙骑车免费；黄河老堤顺堤走别下水。
Handy tip:The wall bike is free; walk the old river embankment, don't enter water.

The Gui德府 wall is a free path; bike the ring and the four gates, crenels, moat all line the way. The inner alleys are yours to wander; more stories behind closed blue-brick doors than open.

The old river embankment is free too; at dusk it dyes the water orange, better by bike. Shangqiu's joy is the round town stringing the city on one line.

实用贴士：城墙骑车免费；黄河老堤顺堤走别下水。
Handy tip:The wall bike is free; walk the old river embankment, don't enter water.
10

不花钱也值得Worth it free

商丘最贵的东西，大多不要钱。城墙的风、圆城在暮色里的轮廓、古城外那阵豫东调，站着看就是参与。雪后圆城配红灯，比收费园里还好看。

城内大爷们下棋不收钱，你站着看就是捧场。商丘教会我：好地方不靠门票证明自己。

当地贴士：城墙步道免费；博物馆多周一闭馆，去前查开放日。
Local tip:The wall path is free; many museums close Mondays — check first.

Shangqiu's priciest things mostly cost nothing. The wall's wind, the round town's dusk outline, the east-Henan tune beyond — stand and you're in it. Snow on the round town with red lamps beats any paid garden.

Old men play chess free inside; stand and watch, that's applause. Shangqiu taught me: good places don't prove themselves with a ticket.

当地贴士：城墙步道免费；博物馆多周一闭馆，去前查开放日。
Local tip:The wall path is free; many museums close Mondays — check first.
11

收束Before you leave

商丘不会一下子把你看服。它没有名山的名气，也没有水乡的精巧。它圆，它旧，它又商又王——三个"商"能从这出发，一座城能把三千年摁成一圈。

你逛过归德府的一圈圆墙、芒砀山的一道深墓、古城外的一阵豫东调，临走会明白：这片土地最舍得给的，从来不是名气，而是那种"圆能聚气"的稳。它不急着留你，却总让你惦记。

Shangqiu won't win you at once. No famous mountain's fame, no water town's finesse. It's round, old, merchant and king — three 'shang' set out here, one town pressed three thousand years to a ring.

You'll see the round wall's ring, Mangdang's deep tomb, the east-Henan tune beyond — and leaving you'll get it: what this land gives most freely was never fame, but the steadiness of 'round gathers qi'. It won't rush to keep you, yet always leaves you missing it.
