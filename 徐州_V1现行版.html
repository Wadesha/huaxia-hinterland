徐州

Xuzhou

一座把汉朝埋在地下、把日子过在湖边的城——铁路在此交叉，你得下车看一眼。

A city that buried the Han underground and lives its days by the lake — two railways cross here, and you should step off and look.

01

到站先别急着找景点Don't rush for the sights

徐州东站的站台很长，你从车上下来，风里带一点煤灰味，也带一点面香。

这座城不靠好看取胜，它的本事是"通"——京沪和陇海两条铁路在这儿交叉，南来北往的人都得经过。

你先坐一次公交吧，票价便宜，路线绕，正好把老城和新区串起来看一遍。

别急着奔景点。

徐州的好东西大多埋在地下，或者藏在一碗汤里。

The platform at Xuzhou East is long, and when you step off the train the air carries a trace of coal dust and a trace of bread.

This city does not win on looks; its talent is being a junction, where the Beijing–Shanghai and Longhai lines cross and anyone heading north or south passes through.

Take a bus first — fares are cheap, the route wanders, and you get the old town and the new district in one loop.

Don't rush for the sights.

Most of what Xuzhou keeps is underground, or in a bowl of soup.

02

山肚子里是空的The hill is hollow

龟山不高，你爬上去只用几分钟。

可山肚子里是空的，这是一座西汉楚王的墓，直接从山体里凿出来。

两条甬道又长又直，墙壁打磨得很平。

你走在墓道里，头顶是整块石头，两千多年前的工匠一锤一凿把这座山掏成了房子。

出来以后天光刺眼，你会愣一下，才想起刚才在地下待了多久。

Turtle Hill is not high; the climb takes you only a few minutes.

But the hill is hollow — this is the tomb of a Western Han king of Chu, cut straight out of the rock.

Two passageways run long and true, their walls smoothed flat.

You walk the corridor with solid stone overhead; craftsmen more than two thousand years ago hammered this hill into a house.

Daylight is sharp when you come out, and you pause a moment, remembering how long you were underground.

03

四千个小兵Four thousand small soldiers

狮子山楚王陵外有一片俑坑，兵马俑排成方阵，一个个只有几十厘米高。

它们没有西安那种气势。

可你蹲下来看，每张脸都不一样。

这是西汉楚国自己的军队，缩成陶土，替一位王守了两千年。

展馆里人不多，你可以慢慢看那些发式、铠甲，还有马。

Outside the Chu royal tomb at Lion Hill lies a pit of figurines, drawn up in formation, each only a few dozen centimetres tall.

They lack the grandeur of Xi'an.

But when you crouch down, every face is different.

This was the army of the Han state of Chu, shrunk into clay, keeping watch for a king for two thousand years.

The hall is rarely crowded, so you can take your time over the hairstyles, the armour, the horses.

04

云龙湖：一湖水压住了城Yunlong Lake holds the city down

云龙湖很大，你沿湖走，一边是水，一边是云龙山。

本地人在湖边钓鱼、遛娃、跳舞，你加入哪一样都不突兀。

山上有放鹤亭。

苏轼在这儿做过官，写过文章，说鹤飞走了就不回来了。

山不深，林子密，你走到半山腰回头，能看见整片水面。

Yunlong Lake is big; walk along it and there is water on one side, Cloud Dragon Hill on the other.

Locals fish, walk children and dance by the water, and nothing looks odd if you join in.

On the hill stands a pavilion for releasing cranes.

Su Shi served here and wrote about it; the cranes, he said, flew off and never came back.

The hill is not deep and the trees are thick — halfway up, turn around and the whole lake is below you.

05

户部山的老房子Old houses on Hubu Hill

户部山是一片老民居，青砖、小院、窄巷子，抬头是翘起来的屋脊。

崔家大院值得进去，院子一套套往里套，像迷宫。

你走累了就坐在门槛上，有老人晒太阳，有猫从墙头过去。

回龙窝在旁边，修得新一些。

入夜灯亮起来好看。

Hubu Hill is a quarter of old houses: grey brick, small courtyards, narrow lanes, and roof ridges that curl upward.

The Cui family compound is worth going in, courtyard opening into courtyard like a maze.

You can sit on a doorstep when your legs give out; old men take the sun and a cat crosses the wall.

Huilongwo is next door, restored more recently.

It is at its best once the lamps come on.

06

戏马台：项羽在这儿遛过马Where Xiang Yu drilled his horses

项羽定都彭城，在城南的山岗上筑台看兵马操练。

那地方现在叫戏马台，台不高，你站上去看得见一片屋顶。

楚汉相争的故事，本地人讲得比书熟。

谁赢谁输，他们各有各的说法。

台下有石刻和碑，风吹过，树响，别的就安静了。

Xiang Yu made Pengcheng his capital and raised a terrace on the ridge south of town to watch his horses drill.

The place is still called Ximatai; the terrace is not high, and you stand on it and see a field of rooftops.

Locals tell the Chu–Han contest better than books do.

Who won, who lost — everyone has a version.

Below are stone carvings and steles; the wind moves the trees, and everything else goes quiet.

07

城市最近的一道疤The city's most recent scar

城南有一座纪念塔和一座纪念馆。

名字很重：淮海战役。

1948 年的冬天到 1949 年的年初，这里打了六十多天。

你进馆里看地图和旧照片，人很多，声音会低下去。

出来是一片林子和台阶，孩子在上面跑，老人在下面坐。

South of the centre stand a memorial tower and a museum.

The name is heavy: the Huaihai Campaign.

From the winter of 1948 into the first days of 1949, the fighting here ran more than sixty days.

You go in to look at maps and old photographs; crowds come and voices drop.

Outside are trees and steps, children running above and old men sitting below.

08

黄河走了，留下一道故道The river left, and left a bed behind

黄河曾经从这里走，1855 年它改道北上，留下一道干掉的老河床。

现在故道上种了果树，修了路，你骑车过去，路面很平。

潘安湖更特别。

那儿原本是煤矿塌陷地，地沉下去，积了水，后来做成湿地公园。

一片塌陷区能变回一片有鸟的水面，这件事本身就值得看。

The Yellow River once ran through here; in 1855 it shifted north and left behind a dry old bed.

The old course is planted with fruit trees and laid with paths, and you cycle it on a surface that is perfectly flat.

Pan'an Lake is stranger still.

It was mine subsidence — the ground sank and water gathered — and it has since been made into a wetland park.

That a collapsed pit can become a lake full of birds is worth seeing on its own.

09

地锅鸡和一碗啥汤Pot chicken and a bowl of sha tang

徐州的吃很实在，地锅鸡是一口铁锅，鸡肉贴着锅边，饼子半泡在汤里。

啥汤要趁热喝，胡椒味重，配油条或者烙馍。

入伏以后本地人吃伏羊，一碗羊汤下去，出一身汗。

你不一定都吃得惯。

但量足，价实，不会让你饿着走。

实用贴士：啥汤（读 shá）多为早点摊，卖完就收摊，起早才吃得到；地锅鸡在城郊的店做得更足，市区也有不少分店。
Handy tip:Sha tang is sold at breakfast stalls that close once the pot is empty — come early. Pot chicken is heartier at places on the outskirts, though the city has plenty of branches.

Xuzhou food is solid; pot chicken comes in an iron pan, the meat against the rim, flatbread half-drowned in the sauce.

Sha tang should be drunk hot, heavy on pepper, with fried dough or a griddle cake.

When the dog days arrive, locals eat mutton, and one bowl leaves you sweating.

You may not take to all of it.

But portions are generous, prices fair, and you will not leave hungry.

实用贴士：啥汤（读 shá）多为早点摊，卖完就收摊，起早才吃得到；地锅鸡在城郊的店做得更足，市区也有不少分店。
Handy tip:Sha tang is sold at breakfast stalls that close once the pot is empty — come early. Pot chicken is heartier at places on the outskirts, though the city has plenty of branches.
10

几件要紧事A few things that matter

徐州的景点分得散，汉墓在西北，湖在西南，纪念塔在南边。

你按片区看，别来回穿城。

火车站有两个，徐州东站跑高铁，老徐州站离老城近，买票前先看清楚是哪一个。

城里共享单车多，短途骑最省事，夏天晒，冬天风硬。

博物馆值得一去，徐州博物馆和汉画像石馆挨着，能看半天。

实用贴士：徐州博物馆与汉画像石艺术馆相邻，凭身份证可领免费票；狮子山楚王陵、龟山汉墓在西北郊，公交可达但班次不密，往返要留富余时间。
Handy tip:The Xuzhou Museum and the Han Stone Relief art gallery are neighbours — bring ID for a free ticket. The Chu tomb at Lion Hill and the Turtle Hill tomb are in the northwest suburbs: buses reach them, but not often, so allow slack.

Xuzhou's sights are spread out — the Han tombs in the northwest, the lake in the southwest, the memorial in the south.

Take them by district; don't keep crossing the city.

There are two railway stations: Xuzhou East takes the high-speed trains, the old Xuzhou station is closer to the old town, so check which one before you buy.

Shared bikes are everywhere and best for short hops; summers are hot, and winters have a hard wind.

The museums are worth it — the Xuzhou Museum and the Han stone-relief gallery sit side by side and can fill half a day.

实用贴士：徐州博物馆与汉画像石艺术馆相邻，凭身份证可领免费票；狮子山楚王陵、龟山汉墓在西北郊，公交可达但班次不密，往返要留富余时间。
Handy tip:The Xuzhou Museum and the Han Stone Relief art gallery are neighbours — bring ID for a free ticket. The Chu tomb at Lion Hill and the Turtle Hill tomb are in the northwest suburbs: buses reach them, but not often, so allow slack.
11

离开的时候On leaving

徐州不留人，它太像个路口了，人来人往，谁都只是路过。

可你坐上车以后会发现，那些墓、那些兵、那锅鸡，还在脑子里。

这座城把汉朝埋在地下，把战役写在碑上，把日子过在湖边和汤碗里。

下次再来也行。

不来，也说得过去。

Xuzhou does not hold people; it is too much a crossroads, and everyone is only passing through.

But once you are on the train, the tombs, the soldiers, that pan of chicken — they stay with you.

This city buried the Han underground, wrote its battles on stone, and lives its days by the lake and in a bowl of soup.

Come again, fine.

Never come back, also fine.
