淮北

Huaibei

一座挖煤挖出来的城，地下有矿，地上有茶馆——塌陷积水成了湖，老镇上的茶炉还没熄。

A city dug out of coal: mines below, teahouses above, and the water that filled the hollows has become lakes.

01

先看这座城是怎么来的How this city came to exist

淮北是安徽最北的地级市，旁边就是河南和山东。

它很年轻，因煤而建，1960 年才设市。

你在城里走，能看到很多"矿"字头的地名和单位。

这地方平原为主，山只有相山那一小片。

城不大，走路加公交就能转完，别怕迷路。

Huaibei is Anhui's northernmost prefecture-level city, with Henan and Shandong close by.

It is young, built on coal, and only became a city in 1960.

Walk around and you keep hitting place names and work units with "mine" in them.

The land is mostly plain; the only real hills are the small Xiangshan range.

The city is not large — walking plus buses covers it, so don't fear getting lost.

02

相山：城边唯一的一片高Xiangshan: the only high ground

相山在城西北，海拔不高，却是这片平原上少见的起伏。

山上有庙，也有碑，树长得密。

你爬到半山腰回头，能看见整座城铺在下面。

本地人周末来这儿遛弯、打牌、带孩子。

山不高，但它是这座城的制高点，也是唯一的""景""。

Xiangshan rises northwest of town, low, but it is rare unevenness on this plain.

There is a temple on it, and steles, and dense trees.

Halfway up, turn around: the whole city is laid out below you.

Locals come at weekends to stroll, play cards and bring their children.

It is not high, but it is the city's high point and its only real "sight".

03

临涣：一座被茶泡着的古镇Linhuan, a town steeped in tea

临涣在濉溪，离市区有段路，是座老镇。

镇上有好几家老茶馆，房子旧，炉子老，桌子磨得发亮。

这里喝的是棒棒茶，用茶梗煮，颜色深，味道粗。

你坐下来，一壶几块钱，坐多久都行。

满屋子都是本地口音，没人看你，也没人赶你。

Linhuan is in Suixi county, some way from the city, and it is an old town.

It has several old teahouses: old buildings, old stoves, tables worn shiny.

What they serve is stick tea, brewed from tea stems, dark in the cup and rough on the tongue.

Sit down and a pot costs a few yuan, and you can stay as long as you like.

The room is full of local speech; nobody looks at you and nobody moves you on.

04

茶馆里的时间Time inside a teahouse

临涣的茶馆开门很早，天没亮就有人来。

老人端着杯子聊天，话题从收成到孙子，什么都聊。

你要是不赶时间，就点一壶，坐着听。

茶粗，水烫，话多。

这种地方不卖体验，它就是日子本身。

The teahouses at Linhuan open early — people arrive before it is properly light.

Old men hold their cups and talk, about the harvest and about grandsons and about everything else.

If you are not in a hurry, order a pot and sit and listen.

The tea is coarse, the water is scalding, and the talk is endless.

A place like this does not sell an experience; it is simply the day itself.

05

柳孜：运河埋在地底下Liuzi: the canal under the fields

濉溪的柳孜村底下埋着一段隋唐大运河。

二十世纪末考古挖出了石构码头、沉船和成堆的瓷器。

现场建了馆，你能看到当年河道的位置和出土的船。

站在那儿看，脚下这片平地从前是一条繁忙的水道。

现在只剩麦地和村路，看不出一点水的痕迹。

Under Liuzi village in Suixi lies a stretch of the Sui–Tang Grand Canal.

Excavation at the end of the last century uncovered a stone quay, sunken boats and heaps of porcelain.

A museum stands on the site, showing where the channel ran and the boats that came out of it.

Stand there and consider that this flat ground was once a busy waterway.

Now there are wheat fields and village roads, and not a trace of water.

06

塌陷区变成了湖The hollows became lakes

挖煤会把地面挖沉，沉下去就积水。

淮北城边有好几片这样的水面，现在都修成了湿地公园。

你在湖边走，芦苇长得很高，鸟也多。

南湖、中湖都是这么来的。

一片塌陷地，最后变成城里最舒服的地方，这事挺有意思。

Mining makes the ground sink, and what sinks fills with water.

There are several such sheets of water around Huaibei, all of them now made into wetland parks.

Walk along them: the reeds are tall and the birds are plentiful.

The South Lake and the Central Lake both began this way.

That a collapsed pit should end up the pleasantest place in the city is a good joke on the place.

07

双堆集：一次围歼Shuangduiji: a battle of encirclement

双堆集在濉溪，是个村子。

1948 年冬天，这里打过一场硬仗，是淮海战役的一部分。

村外有烈士陵园和纪念馆，塔立在地平线上很远就看得见。

你进去看展板，地形图上是密密麻麻的箭头。

出来是一片麦田，安静得有点突然。

Shuangduiji is a village in Suixi.

In the winter of 1948 a hard battle was fought here, part of the Huaihai Campaign.

Outside the village stand a memorial park and a museum, the tower visible from far off across the flat land.

Go in and read the panels; the maps are dense with arrows.

Outside there are wheat fields, quiet enough to be startling.

08

酒与面Liquor and wheat

濉溪出酒，口子窖是本地的名字，街上到处是招牌。

本地人喝酒实在，杯子小，次数多。

吃的以面食为主，面条、烙馍、水煎包。

你可以去早市吃一碗汤，配馍，几块钱管饱。

这里不讲究精致，讲究实在。

实用贴士：临涣茶馆集中在老街一带，一壶棒棒茶几块钱，续水不另收；去之前别吃太饱，本地早点分量足。
Handy tip:The Linhuan teahouses cluster on the old street; a pot of stick tea costs a few yuan and refills are free. Don't arrive too full — local breakfasts are large.

Suixi makes liquor, and Kouzijiao is the local name, splashed across shopfronts everywhere.

People here drink steadily rather than showily: small glasses, many rounds.

The food is wheat-based — noodles, griddle cakes, pan-fried buns.

Go to an early market for a bowl of soup with bread; a few yuan and you are full.

Refinement is not the point here; substance is.

09

一座在改行的城A city changing trade

煤挖了六十年，浅的挖完了，深的不好挖。

这座城正在想别的路子：新材料、食品、旅游。

你在新区能看到新的厂房和新的路。

老城区慢一些，人也松一些。

转型这种事说起来大，走起来就是一条街一条街地改。

Coal has been dug here for sixty years; the shallow seams are gone and the deep ones are awkward.

The city is looking for other lines of work: new materials, food processing, tourism.

In the new district you see new factory buildings and new roads.

The old centre is slower, and the people are looser.

"Restructuring" sounds grand, but on the ground it is one street at a time.

几件要紧事A few things that matter

淮北的看点都在市区外：临涣在南边，柳孜和双堆集在濉溪。

你最好包车或者坐班车，公交班次少。

高铁站是淮北北站，离市区有点远。

城里消费低，吃住都不贵。

夏天热，冬天干冷，秋天最舒服。

实用贴士：临涣、柳孜、双堆集三处方向不同，一天走完要包车；柳孜遗址博物馆以遗迹与出土器物为主，参观前先看一眼展陈介绍更容易看懂。
Handy tip:Linhuan, Liuzi and Shuangduiji lie in different directions, so covering them in one day means hiring a car. The museum at Liuzi is about the remains and the finds — read the introduction first and the site makes far more sense.

Everything worth seeing is outside the city: Linhuan to the south, Liuzi and Shuangduiji in Suixi county.

Hire a car or take a coach; the buses are infrequent.

The high-speed station is Huaibei North, some distance from town.

Prices in the city are low, and neither eating nor sleeping costs much.

Summers are hot and winters dry and cold; autumn is the comfortable season.

11

离开的时候On leaving

淮北不是旅游城市，它自己也知道。

可你坐一会儿茶馆，看看塌陷湖，会记下点东西。

一座城把自己挖沉，又把沉下去的地方改成公园。

这种修补比风景更有意思。

走的时候你会想，这地方挺实在。

Huaibei is not a tourist city, and it knows it.

But sit in a teahouse for a while, look at the sunken lakes, and something stays with you.

A city dug itself into a hole, then turned the hole into a park.

That repair is more interesting than any scenery.

You leave thinking: this is a solid sort of place.
